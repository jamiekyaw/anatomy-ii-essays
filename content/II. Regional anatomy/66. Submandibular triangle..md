> Submandibular triangle.

---

# 1. Borders (The most important part)
- **Superior:** Inferior border of the Mandible (jawbone).
- **Anterior:** Anterior belly of the Digastric muscle.
- **Posterior:** Posterior belly of the Digastric muscle.

![|400](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/36/Musculi_coli_base%2C_my_edits_for_tringles%2C_labeled_triangles%2C_Submandib.svg)

# 2. Floor (What lies underneath)
- **Mylohyoid muscle** (Anteriorly).
- **Hyoglossus muscle** (Posteriorly).

# 3. Key Contents (Top 5)
1. **Submandibular Gland:** (The largest structure in the triangle).
2. **Submandibular Lymph Nodes:** (Clinical importance for infections/cancer).
3. **Facial Artery and Vein:** (Main blood supply/drainage).
4. **Hypoglossal Nerve (Cranial Nerve XII):** (Motor supply to the tongue).
5. **Nerve to Mylohyoid.**
