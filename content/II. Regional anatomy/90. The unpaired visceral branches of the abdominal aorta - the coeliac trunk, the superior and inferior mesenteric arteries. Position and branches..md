> The unpaired visceral branches of the abdominal aorta - the coeliac trunk, the superior and inferior mesenteric arteries. Position and branches.

---

# 1. Coeliac Trunk
- **Vertebral Level:** **T12** (highest of the three).
- **Supplies:** The **Foregut** (Stomach, liver, spleen, first half of duodenum).
- **3 Main Branches:**
    1. **Left Gastric Artery:** Supplies the stomach (lesser curvature).
    2. **Splenic Artery:** Supplies the spleen and pancreas (runs along the upper border of the pancreas).
    3. **Common Hepatic Artery:** Supplies the liver and gallbladder.

---

# 2. Superior Mesenteric Artery (SMA)
- **Vertebral Level:** **L1** (just below the celiac trunk).
- **Supplies:** The **Midgut** (Lower duodenum to the first 2/3 of the transverse colon).
- **Main Branches:**
    - **Inferior Pancreaticoduodenal:** Head of the pancreas.
    - **Jejunal and Ileal Arteries:** Small intestine.
    - **Ileocolic Artery:** Cecum and appendix.
    - **Right Colic Artery:** Ascending colon.
    - **Middle Colic Artery:** Transverse colon.

![|500](https://www.earthslab.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Superior-mesenteric-artery-Course.jpg)

---

# 3. Inferior Mesenteric Artery (IMA)
- **Vertebral Level:** **L3** (at the level of the umbilicus).
- **Supplies:** The **Hindgut** (Distal 1/3 of the transverse colon to the rectum).
- **Main Branches:**
    1. **Left Colic Artery:** Descending colon.
    2. **Sigmoid Arteries:** Sigmoid colon.
    3. **Superior Rectal Artery:** Top part of the rectum.
