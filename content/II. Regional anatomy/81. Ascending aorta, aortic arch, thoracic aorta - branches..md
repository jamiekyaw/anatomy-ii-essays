> Ascending aorta, aortic arch, thoracic aorta - branches.

---

# 1. Ascending Aorta
This is the very first part of the aorta leaving the heart.
- **Branches:** **Right and Left Coronary Arteries.**
- **Supplies:** The heart muscle itself.

![|300](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Gray506.svg)

---

# 2. Aortic Arch
This is the "curve" at the top.

There are **3 main branches** you must know (from right to left). Use the mnemonic **ABC'S**:

1. **B**rachiocephalic Trunk
	- (This then splits into the Right Common Carotid and Right Subclavian)
2. **L**eft **C**ommon Carotid Artery.
3. **L**eft **S**ubclavian Artery.
- **Supplies:** Head, neck, and upper limbs.

![|300](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Gray506.svg)

---

# 3. Thoracic Aorta (Descending)
This is the part that runs down through the chest (from vertebrae T4 to T12).

- **Posterior Intercostal Arteries:** Supply the ribs and chest wall.
- **Bronchial Arteries:** Supply the lungs/bronchi.
- **Esophageal Arteries:** Supply the esophagus.
- **Superior Phrenic Arteries:** Supply the top of the diaphragm.

---

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Ascending:** Coronary arteries.
- **Arch:** Brachiocephalic, Left Common Carotid, Left Subclavian.
- **Thoracic:** Intercostal (ribs), Bronchial (lungs), Esophageal (esophagus).
