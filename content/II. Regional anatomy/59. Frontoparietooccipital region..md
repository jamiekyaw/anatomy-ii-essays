> Frontoparietooccipital region.

---

# 1. The Layers (Mnemonic: S-C-A-L-P)
1. **Skin:** 
	- Thick, contains hair follicles and sebaceous glands.
2. **Connective Tissue (Dense):** 
	- Contains blood vessels and nerves. 
3. **Aponeurosis (Galea Aponeurotica):** 
	- A thin, tough sheet of tendon connecting the Frontalis (front) and Occipitalis (back) muscles.
4. **Loose Areolar Tissue:** 
	- The **"Dangerous Layer."** 
5. **Pericranium:** 
	- The outer layer (periosteum) of the skull bones.

---

# 2. Muscles
- **Occipitofrontalis:** 
    - **Frontal belly:** Raises eyebrows/wrinkles forehead.
    - **Occipital belly:** Pulls scalp backward.
    - Joined by the **Galea Aponeurotica**.

---

# 3. Nerve & Blood Supply
- **Arteries:** Mainly branches of the **External Carotid Artery** (Superficial temporal, Posterior auricular, Occipital).
- **Nerve:** 
    - **Motor:** Facial Nerve (CN VII) – moves the muscles.
    - **Sensory:** Trigeminal Nerve (CN V) – feel sensation on the top/front.

***

**Summary for the Examiner:** 
*"The frontoparietooccipital region is the scalp. It has five layers: Skin, Connective tissue, Aponeurosis, Loose tissue, and Pericranium. The loose tissue is the 'dangerous layer' because emissary veins can carry infection from the scalp to the intracranial dural sinuses."*
