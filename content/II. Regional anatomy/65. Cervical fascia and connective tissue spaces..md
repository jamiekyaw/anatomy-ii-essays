> Cervical fascia and connective tissue spaces.

---

# 1. Superficial Cervical Fascia and Deep Cervical Fascia

**Superficial Cervical Fascia:**
- **Location:** Just under the skin.
- **Key Content:** The **Platysma muscle**.

**Deep Cervical Fascia:**
Memory trick: **I.P.P.**

1. **Investing Layer:**
    - The most superficial "sleeve" of the deep fascia.
    - Surrounds the whole neck.
    - **Key Fact:** It splits to enclose the **Sternocleidomastoid (SCM)** and **Trapezius** muscles.

2. **Pretracheal Layer:**
    - Located only in the anterior neck.
    - **Key Fact:** It encloses the **Thyroid gland**, trachea, and esophagus.

3. **Prevertebral Layer:**
    - A "sleeve" around the vertebral column and its deep muscles.
    - **Key Fact:** Forms the floor of the posterior triangle of the neck.

# 2. The Carotid Sheath
This is a column of fascia that runs vertically through the neck.
**Contents (The Big 3):**
1. **Common/Internal Carotid Artery**
2. **Internal Jugular Vein**
3. **Vagus Nerve (CN X)**

---

# 3. Deep Neck Spaces

- **Retropharyngeal Space:** 
    - Located between the pharynx and the prevertebral fascia.

- **Danger Space:**
    - A thin space within the prevertebral fascia (between the alar and prevertebral layers).

- **Pretracheal Space:**
    - Located in front of the trachea.

---

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Investing layer** = SCM and Trapezius.
- **Pretracheal layer** = Thyroid and Trachea.
- **Carotid Sheath** = Artery, Vein, Vagus nerve.
- **Retropharyngeal/Danger Space** = Path for infection to reach the chest (Mediastinum).
