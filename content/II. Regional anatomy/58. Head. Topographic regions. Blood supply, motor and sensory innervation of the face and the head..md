> Head. Topographic regions. Blood supply, motor and sensory innervation of the face and the head.

---

# 1. Topographic Regions
The head is divided into the **Neurocranium** (braincase) and **Viscerocranium** (face).

- **Scalp (Mnemonic: SCALP):**
    1. **S**kin
    2. **C**onnective tissue (dense)
    3. **A**poneurosis (Epicranial)
    4. **L**oose connective tissue (**Danger zone**—infection spreads easily here)
    5. **P**ericranium (bone surface)
- **Face Regions:**
	- Orbital (eyes), Nasal (nose), Oral (mouth), Mental (chin), Buccal (cheek), Zygomatic (cheekbone), and Parotid-masseteric (angle of the jaw).

---

# 2. Motor Innervation (Movement)
There are two main nerves you must distinguish:

- **Facial Nerve (CN VII):** Controls all **muscles of facial expression**.
    - *Exit point:* Stylomastoid foramen.
    - *5 Terminal Branches (Mnemonic: **T**en **Z**ulus **B**uggered **M**y **C**at):*
        1. **T**emporal
        2. **Z**ygomatic
        3. **B**uccal
        4. **M**andibular (Marginal)
        5. **C**ervical
- **Trigeminal Nerve - Mandibular Branch (CN V3):** Controls **muscles of mastication** (chewing).
    - Includes: Masseter, Temporalis, Medial & Lateral Pterygoids.

---

# 3. Sensory Innervation (Feeling)
The **Trigeminal Nerve (CN V)** provides almost all sensory input to the face. It splits into three divisions:

1. **Ophthalmic (V1):** Forehead, upper eyelid, and bridge of the nose. (Exits via Supraorbital notch).
2. **Maxillary (V2):** Lower eyelid, cheek, upper lip, and upper teeth. (Exits via Infraorbital foramen).
3. **Mandibular (V3):** Lower lip, chin, lower teeth, and the area in front of the ear. (Exits via Mental foramen).

*Note: The back of the head (occipital region) is supplied by Spinal Nerves (C2, C3), not the Trigeminal.*

---

# 4. Blood Supply (Arteries)
Most blood comes from the **External Carotid Artery (ECA)**.

- **Facial Artery:** Main supply for the face. Pulses can be felt at the lower border of the mandible.
- **Superficial Temporal Artery:** Supplies the scalp and side of the head. Pulse felt in front of the ear.
- **Maxillary Artery:** Deep artery; supplies the teeth, palate, and nose.
- **Ophthalmic Artery:** (Branch of *Internal* Carotid) Supplies the orbit and forehead.

---

# 5. Venous Drainage
- **Facial Vein:** The primary drainage. It joins the retromandibular vein to empty into the **Internal Jugular Vein**.
- **The Danger Triangle:** The area from the corners of the mouth to the bridge of the nose.
    - *Why it matters:* Veins here (Ophthalmic veins) connect directly to the **Cavernous Sinus** inside the skull. Infections from a squeezed pimple here can lead to brain infections (Meningitis/Cavernous Sinus Thrombosis).

---

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
1. **CN VII** = Face muscles (Expression).
2. **CN V3** = Chewing muscles.
3. **CN V (V1, V2, V3)** = Face sensation.
4. **External Carotid** = Main blood supply.
5. **SCALP** = 5 layers of the head.
6. **Danger Triangle** = Risk of infection spreading to the brain.
