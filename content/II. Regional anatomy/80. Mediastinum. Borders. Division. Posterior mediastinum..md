> Mediastinum. Borders. Division. Posterior mediastinum.

---

# 1. Definition
The **mediastinum** is the central compartment of the thoracic cavity, located between the two pleural sacs (lungs).

---

# 2. General Borders
- **Anterior:** Sternum.
- **Posterior:** Thoracic vertebral column (T1–T12).
- **Superior:** Thoracic inlet (root of the neck).
- **Inferior:** Diaphragm.
- **Lateral:** Mediastinal pleura (lungs).

---

# 3. Divisions
The mediastinum is divided into **Superior** and **Inferior** parts by an imaginary horizontal line called the **Transverse Thoracic Plane**.

- **Location of Plane:** From the **Sternal Angle** (Angle of Louis) to the **T4/T5 intervertebral disc**.

## A. Superior Mediastinum
- **Location:** Above the T4/T5 plane.
- **Key Contents:** Arch of aorta, Great vessels (SVC, Brachiocephalic veins), Trachea, Esophagus, Vagus & Phrenic nerves, Thymus (in children).

## B. Inferior Mediastinum
Divided into three parts based on the position of the heart (pericardium):
1. **Anterior:** Between sternum and heart. (Content: Fat, Thymus remnants).
2. **Middle:** Contains the **Heart** and roots of great vessels.
3. **Posterior:** Between heart and vertebrae.

---

# 4. Posterior Mediastinum (High Yield)
This is the area behind the heart and in front of vertebrae T5–T12.

**Key Contents (The "DATES" Mnemonic):**
- **D** – **Descending Aorta** (Thoracic aorta).
- **A** – **Azygos vein** (and hemiazygos).
- **T** – **Thoracic Duct** (main lymph vessel).
- **E** – **Esophagus**.
- **S** – **Sympathetic trunks** (and Splanchnic nerves) + **Vagus nerves**.

---

# 3 Points to Remember for "The Pass":
1. The **Sternal Angle (T4/T5)** is the landmark that divides superior from inferior.
2. The **Middle Mediastinum** is where the heart lives.
3. The **Esophagus and Thoracic Aorta** are the biggest structures in the **Posterior Mediastinum**.
