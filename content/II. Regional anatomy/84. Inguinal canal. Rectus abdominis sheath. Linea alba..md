> Inguinal canal. Rectus abdominis sheath. Linea alba.

---

# 1. Inguinal Canal
A 4cm tunnel in the lower abdomen.

- **Location:** Just above the medial half of the inguinal ligament.
- **Two Openings:** 
    1. **Deep Ring:** Internal opening (in the fascia transversalis).
    2. **Superficial Ring:** External opening (in the external oblique aponeurosis).
- **Key Contents:**
    - **Males:** Spermatic cord.
    - **Females:** Round ligament of the uterus.
    - **Both:** Ilioinguinal nerve.
- **Boundaries (The "MALT" Mnemonic):**
    - **M**uscles (Roof): Internal oblique and Transversus abdominis.
    - **A**poneurosis (Anterior wall): External oblique.
    - **L**igament (Floor): Inguinal ligament.
    - **T**endon/T-fascia (Posterior wall): Conjoint tendon and Fascia transversalis.

# 2. Rectus Abdominis Sheath
A fibrous envelope enclosing the Rectus Abdominis muscle.

- **Formation:** Formed by the fused tendons (aponeuroses) of the 3 flat abdominal muscles (External oblique, Internal oblique, Transversus abdominis).
- **The Arcuate Line (Critical Concept):** 
    - This is a horizontal line found midway between the navel and the pubic bone.
    - **Above the line:** The sheath has a front and back wall (encloses the muscle).
    - **Below the line:** All layers move to the **front**. There is **no posterior wall** below the arcuate line (the muscle touches the fascia transversalis directly).
- **Contents:**
    1. Rectus abdominis muscle.
    2. Pyramidalis muscle (if present).
    3. **Superior and Inferior epigastric vessels** (very high yield).

# 3. Linea Alba
The "White Line" of the abdomen.

- **Definition:** A single midline fibrous band running down the center of the abdomen.
- **Formation:** Created by the fusion of the rectus sheath layers from both the left and right sides.
- **Extent:** From the **Xiphoid process** (top) to the **Pubic symphysis** (bottom).

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Inguinal Canal:** Know it holds the **Spermatic cord** and has a **Deep** and **Superficial** ring.
- **Rectus Sheath:** Know it contains the **Epigastric vessels** and the **Arcuate line** changes its structure.
- **Linea Alba:** Know it is the **midline** where all muscles meet and is used for **surgery** because it doesn't bleed much.
