> Common carotid artery. External and internal carotid artery. Distribution and branches of the external carotid artery in the cervical region.

---

# 1. Common Carotid Artery (CCA)
- **Origin:** 
    - **Right:** From the Brachiocephalic trunk.
    - **Left:** Directly from the Arch of the Aorta.
- **Termination:** Bifurcates (splits) into Internal and External carotid arteries at the **upper border of the thyroid cartilage (C4 level).**
- **Special Structures at the split:**
    - **Carotid Sinus:** A dilation that acts as a **baroreceptor** (senses blood pressure).
    - **Carotid Body:** A small mass that acts as a **chemoreceptor** (senses oxygen levels).

# 2. Internal Carotid Artery (ICA)
- **Key Fact:** It has **NO branches in the neck.**
- **Course:** Enters the skull through the **carotid canal**.
- **Distribution:** Supplies the Brain and the Eyes.

# 3. External Carotid Artery (ECA)
- **Distribution:** Supplies structures **outside** the skull (face, scalp, neck, and tongue).
- **Termination:** Ends within the parotid gland by dividing into the Maxillary and Superficial Temporal arteries.

# 4. Branches of the ECA (High Yield)
There are **8 branches**. Use the mnemonic: **"S**ome **A**natomists **L**ike **F**reaking **O**ut **P**oor **M**edical **S**tudents."

1. **S**uperior thyroid artery (supplies thyroid gland).
2. **A**scending pharyngeal artery (supplies the pharynx).
3. **L**ingual artery (supplies the tongue).
4. **F**acial artery (supplies the face).
5. **O**ccipital artery (supplies back of the scalp).
6. **P**osterior auricular artery (supplies skin behind the ear).
7. **M**axillary artery (Terminal branch; supplies deep face/teeth).
8. **S**uperficial temporal artery (Terminal branch; supplies the side of the scalp).

# Summary Table for the Exam
| Feature               | Internal Carotid (ICA) | External Carotid (ECA)          |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| **Branches in Neck**  | None                   | Yes (6 cervical branches)       |
| **Position at start** | Postero-lateral        | Antero-medial                   |
| **Supplies**          | Brain, Eyes            | Face, Scalp, Neck, Tongue       |
| **Terminal Branches** | Middle/Ant. Cerebral   | Maxillary, Superficial Temporal |
