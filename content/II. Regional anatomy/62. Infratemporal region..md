> Infratemporal region.

---

# 1. Location
- It is the space located **deep to the ramus of the mandible** and **below the zygomatic arch.**

# 2. Boundaries (The Walls)
- **Lateral (Outer):** Ramus of the mandible.
- **Medial (Inner):** Lateral pterygoid plate (part of the sphenoid bone).
- **Anterior (Front):** Posterior surface of the Maxilla.
- **Roof:** Greater wing of the sphenoid bone.

# 3. Contents
**Muscles of Mastication:**
- **Lateral Pterygoid**
- **Medial Pterygoid**

**Nerves:**
- **Mandibular Nerve:** The main nerve of this area.
- **Lingual Nerve:** Provides sensation to the tongue.
- **Inferior Alveolar Nerve:** Goes into the lower jaw/teeth.
- **Chorda Tympani:** A branch of the Facial Nerve that joins the lingual nerve.
- **Otic Ganglion:** A small nerve knot (parasympathetic) located just below the foramen ovale.

**Vessels:**
- **Maxillary Artery:** The major artery supplying the deep face.
- **Pterygoid Venous Plexus:**
	- A network of veins (important because it communicates with the cavernous sinus in the brain).

# 4. Communications (How it connects)
- **To the Cheek:** Via the gap between the buccinator and superior constrictor.
- **To the Orbit:** Via the Inferior Orbital Fissure.
- **To the Pterygopalatine Fossa:** Via the Pterygomaxillary Fissure.

# Summary Cheat Sheet
If they ask for "Contents," just remember: **"Two muscles (Pterygoids), Two nerves (Lingual/Alveolar), and One big artery (Maxillary)."**
