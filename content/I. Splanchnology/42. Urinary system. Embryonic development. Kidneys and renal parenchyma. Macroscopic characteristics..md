> Urinary system. Embryonic development. Kidneys and renal parenchyma. Macroscopic characteristics.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:**
	* All develop from the **intermediate mesoderm**.
- **Three Stages (Cranial to Caudal):**
    - **Pronephros:**
	    - Non-functional
	    - Disappears quickly
    - **Mesonephros:**
	    - Functions briefly in early gestation
	    - Most disappears (remnants form male genital ducts).
    - **Metanephros:**
	    - The **permanent kidney**. Starts developing in week 5.
- **Metanephros Components:**
    - **Ureteric Bud:**
	    - Forms the "plumbing"
		    - Ureter, renal pelvis, calyces, collecting ducts.
    - **Metanephrogenic Blastema:**
	    - Forms the "filters"
		    - Nephrons: Bowman’s capsule, proximal/distal tubules, Loop of Henle.
- **Positional Change:** Kidneys ascend from the pelvis to the upper abdomen.

# 2. Macroscopic Characteristics
- **Location:** Retroperitoneal behind the peritoneum at the level **T12 to L3**.
- **The Right Kidney:** Usually lower than the left due to the liver.
- **The Hilum (Entrance/Exit):**
	- Found on the medial border. Order from anterior to posterior:
    1. Renal Vein
    2. Renal Artery
    3. Renal Pelvis / Ureter
- **Protective Layers from inside to outside):**
    1. Renal Capsule (fibrous)
    2. Perirenal Fat
    3. Renal Fascia (Gerota’s fascia)
    4. Pararenal Fat

# 3. Renal Parenchyma
The functional tissue of the kidney is divided into two distinct zones:

- **Cortex: (90% of the kidney's blood supply goes)**
    - Outer layer
    - Produces the hormone erythropoietin
    - Houses approximately one million microscopic filters called **nephrons** 
	    - Comprising **glomeruli** and **tubules**
	    - The **glomeruli** here **filter** out toxins and waste from the blood.
    - **Renal Columns of Bertin:** 
	    - **Inward extensions** of the renal cortex that project down between the pyramids in medulla.
- **Medulla:**
    - Inner layer
    - Consists of 8–18 **Renal Pyramids** (triangle structures).
    - Contains the **Loops of Henle** and **Collecting Ducts**.
    - **Renal Papilla:** Apex of the pyramid that points toward the center.
	    - **Final exit point** where urine leaves the kidney tissue and empties into the **minor calyces**
