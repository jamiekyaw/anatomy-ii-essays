> Male reproductive system. Ductus deferens. Seminal vesicles. Prostate gland. Bulbourethral glands. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Ductus Deferens (Vas Deferens)
- **Macroscopic:** 
	- Long, muscular tube; part of the spermatic cord; ends in an enlargement called the **ampulla**.
- **Microscopic:** 
    - **Lumen:** Narrow and star-shaped.
    - **Epithelium:** Pseudostratified columnar with **stereocilia**.
    - **Muscularis (Key Feature):** Extremely thick, 3 layers (**Inner Longitudinal, Middle Circular, Outer Longitudinal**).
- **Ultramicroscopic:** 
	- Cells have long microvilli (stereocilia) to absorb fluid.
- **Function:** 
	- Rapid transport of sperm from the epididymis to the ejaculatory duct via peristalsis.
- **Blood Supply:** Artery to ductus deferens (branch of Superior Vesical Artery).
- **Nerve Supply:** Sympathetic fibers from the pelvic plexus (drives ejaculation).

# 2. Seminal Vesicles
- **Macroscopic:** 
	- Paired, highly coiled tubular glands located behind the bladder.
- **Microscopic:** 
    - Mucosa is highly folded (honeycomb appearance).
    - Epithelium: Pseudostratified columnar or simple columnar.
- **Ultramicroscopic:** 
	- Rich in Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER) and Golgi (protein-secreting cells).
- **Function:** 
	- Secretes 70% of semen volume. Fluid is alkaline and contains **Fructose** (energy for sperm) and **Prostaglandins**.
- **Blood Supply:** Inferior vesical and middle rectal arteries.
- **Nerve Supply:** Sympathetic (for contraction) and Parasympathetic (for secretion) via pelvic plexus.

# 3. Prostate Gland
- **Macroscopic:** 
	- Walnut-sized gland surrounding the prostatic urethra, inferior to the bladder.
- **Microscopic:** 
    - **Stroma:** Dense fibromuscular stroma (smooth muscle + connective tissue).
    - **Glands:** Tubuloalveolar glands.
    - **Hallmark:** **Corpora amylacea** (calcified "prostatic stones" in the lumen—increases with age).
- **Ultramicroscopic:** 
	- Abundant secretory granules containing enzymes.
- **Function:** 
	- Secretes 30% of semen. Fluid contains **PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)** to liquefy semen and **Acid Phosphatase**.
- **Blood Supply:** Inferior vesical and middle rectal arteries.
- **Nerve Supply:** Prostatic plexus (Sympathetic for contraction; Parasympathetic for secretion).

# 4. Bulbourethral Glands (Cowper’s Glands)
- **Macroscopic:** 
	- Two pea-sized glands located in the deep perineal pouch (below the prostate).
- **Microscopic:** 
	- Compound tubuloalveolar glands. Mucus-secreting cells with pale cytoplasm.
- **Ultramicroscopic:** 
	- Large mucinogen droplets in the apical cytoplasm.
- **Function:** 
	- Secretes "pre-ejaculate" (clear, slippery fluid) to **lubricate** the urethra and **neutralize** acidic urine residue.
- **Blood Supply:** Internal pudendal artery.
- **Nerve Supply:** Parasympathetic fibers from the pelvic plexus.
