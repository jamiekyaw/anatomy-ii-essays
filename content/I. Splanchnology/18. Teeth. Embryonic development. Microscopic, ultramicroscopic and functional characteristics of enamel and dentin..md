> Teeth. Embryonic development. Microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics of enamel and dentin.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development (Odontogenesis)

**1. Primary Germ Layers**
- **Ectoderm:**
	- Forms the **Enamel Organ** (leads to Enamel).
- **Ectomesenchyme (Neural Crest):**
	- Forms **Dental Papilla** (Dentin/Pulp) and **Dental Follicle** (Cementum/PDL).

**2. Morphological Stages**
1. **Bud Stage:** Dental lamina invaginates into mesenchyme.
2. **Cap Stage:** Formation of the Enamel Organ.
3. **Bell Stage:**
    - *Histodifferentiation:* Cells become specialized (Ameloblasts & Odontoblasts).
    - *Morphodifferentiation:* The crown takes its final shape.
4. **Apposition:** Deposition of the matrix of hard tissues.
5. **Maturation:** Mineralization of the matrix.

# 2. Enamel (The Hardest Tissue)

- **Origin:** Ectoderm (Ameloblasts).
- **Composition:** 96% Inorganic (Hydroxyapatite), 4% Organic (Proteins/Water). **Non-vital** (cannot repair itself).

**1. Microscopic Characteristics**
- **Enamel Rods (Prisms)**
- **Neonatal Line**
- **Hunter-Schreger Bands**
- **Enamel Tufts & Lamellae**
- **Enamel Spindles**

**2. Ultramicroscopic**
- Crystalline lattice of **Hydroxyapatite**.
- Crystals in the "head" of the rod run parallel to its long axis; "tail" crystals run obliquely.

**3. Function**
- Resists masticatory forces (wear resistance).
- Chemical protection of the tooth core.

---

# 3. Dentin (The Vital Foundation)

- **Origin:** Ectomesenchyme (Odontoblasts).
- **Composition:** 70% Inorganic, 20% Organic (mostly **Type I Collagen**), 10% Water.

**1. Microscopic Characteristics**
- **Dentinal Tubules**
- **Peritubular Dentin:** Highly mineralized wall surrounding the tubule.
- **Intertubular Dentin:** Located between tubules (makes up the bulk of dentin).
- **Lines of Von Ebner**
- **Tomes’ Granular Layer:** Found only in the root (under cementum).

**2. Ultramicroscopic**
- Hydroxyapatite crystals deposited within and between a dense collagen fiber meshwork.

**3. Function**
- **Support:** Provides elastic base for brittle enamel (prevents fractures).
- **Sensory:** Hydrodynamic theory (fluid movement in tubules triggers pain).
- **Defense:** Can form sclerotic dentin (clogged tubules) to block bacteria.

# Summary for Quick Revision

| Feature | Enamel | Dentin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Origin** | Ectoderm (Ameloblasts) | Mesenchyme (Odontoblasts) |
| **Mineral** | 96% (Highest) | 70% |
| **Vitality** | Non-vital (No cells inside) | Vital (Cell processes inside) |
| **Elasticity** | Brittle | Elastic |
| **Unit** | Rods / Prisms | Tubules |
| **Growth Lines** | Striae of Retzius | Lines of Von Ebner |
