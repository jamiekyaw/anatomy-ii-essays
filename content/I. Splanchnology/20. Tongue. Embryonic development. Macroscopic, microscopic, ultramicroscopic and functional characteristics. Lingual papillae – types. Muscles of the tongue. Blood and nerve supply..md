> Tongue. Embryonic development. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Lingual papillae – types. Muscles of the tongue. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Anterior 2/3 (Body):**
	- From **1st Pharyngeal Arch**.
	- Formed by two lateral lingual swellings and one medial swelling (tuberculum impar).
- **Posterior 1/3 (Root):**
	- From **3rd and 4th Pharyngeal Arches**.
	- Formed by the hypobranchial eminence (copula).
- **Muscles:**
	- Derived from **occipital myotomes** (migrate into the tongue).
- **Separation line:**
	- The **Sulcus Terminalis** marks the junction of the oral (anterior) and pharyngeal (posterior) parts.

# 2. Macroscopic Characteristics
- **Parts:**
	- Apex (tip), Body (anterior 2/3), Root (posterior 1/3).
- **Dorsum (Top):**
	- Rough due to papillae.
	- Features the **Sulcus Terminalis** (V-shaped groove)
	- **Foramen Cecum** (remnant of thyroid duct).
- **Ventral (Bottom):**
	- Smooth, thin mucosa.
	- Features the **Frenulum** (midline fold)
	- Deep Lingual Veins (visible through the skin).

# 3. Microscopic & Ultramicroscopic
- **Epithelium:** 
    - Dorsum: Keratinized stratified squamous (on filiform papillae) to non-keratinized.
    - Ventral: Non-keratinized stratified squamous.
- **Lamina Propria:** Dense connective tissue bound directly to muscle (no submucosa on the dorsum).
- **Taste Buds (Ultramicroscopic):** Barrel-shaped structures containing:
    1. **Gustatory cells:** Sensory receptors with microvilli (taste hairs).
    2. **Supporting cells.**
    3. **Basal cells:** Stem cells.

# 4. Lingual Papillae (The 4 Types)
- **Filiform:**
	- Most numerous, cone-shaped, **no taste buds**.
	- Function: Friction/mechanical.
- **Fungiform:**
	- Mushroom-shaped, scattered red dots.
	- Contain taste buds.
- **Vallate (Circumvallate):**
	- Largest, 8–12 in a V-row at the back.
	- Contain many taste buds.
- **Foliate:**
	- Leaf-like folds on the lateral sides.
	- Rudimentary in humans.

# 5. Muscles of the Tongue
All muscles are paired.
- **Intrinsic (Change shape):** Superior longitudinal, Inferior longitudinal, Transverse, Vertical.
- **Extrinsic (Change position):**
    1. **Genioglossus:** Protrudes tongue ("Safety muscle").
    2. **Hyoglossus:** Depresses tongue.
    3. **Styloglossus:** Retracts and elevates.
    4. **Palatoglossus:** Elevates the root.

# 6. Nerve Supply (Crucial for Exams)
- **Motor:** 
    - All muscles by **Hypoglossal Nerve (CN XII)**.
    - *Exception:* Palatoglossus by **Vagus Nerve (CN X)** via pharyngeal plexus.
- **Sensory (Anterior 2/3):** 
    - General (Touch/Temp): **Lingual Nerve** (from CN V3).
    - Special (Taste): **Chorda Tympani** (from CN VII).
- **Sensory (Posterior 1/3):**
    - General + Special (Taste): **Glossopharyngeal Nerve (CN IX)**.

# 7. Blood Supply & Lymphatics
- **Artery:** **Lingual Artery** (branch of External Carotid).
- **Vein:** Deep lingual vein and dorsal lingual veins (drain to Internal Jugular).

# 8. Functional Characteristics
- **Mastication:** Moves food between teeth.
- **Deglutition:** Forms bolus and pushes it into the pharynx.
- **Speech:** Articulation of sounds.
- **Sensory:** Taste (Gustation) and tactile sensation.
