> Larynx. Macroscopic structure.

---

# 1. General Info
- **Location:** Level of **C3–C6** vertebrae.
- **Function:** Airway protection (sphincter), phonation (voice), and respiration.

# 2. The Cartilages (3 Unpaired, 3 Paired)
**Unpaired (The big ones):**
1. **Thyroid:**
	- Shield-shaped. Formed by two laminae. "Adam’s apple" (laryngeal prominence).
2. **Cricoid:**
	- Only **complete ring** of cartilage.
3. **Epiglottis:**
	- Flips down to cover the airway during swallowing.

**Paired (The small ones):**
1. **Arytenoids**
2. **Corniculate & Cuneiform**

# 3. Interior of the Larynx
Divided into three parts by two sets of folds:
1. **Vestibular Folds (False Vocal Cords):** Superior pair. Protective function.
2. **Vocal Folds (True Vocal Cords):** Inferior pair. Responsible for sound.
3. **Glottis:** The opening between the true vocal folds.

**The Spaces:**
- **Vestibule:** Above the vestibular folds.
- **Ventricle:** The pocket between false and true cords.
- **Infraglottic cavity:** Below the true cords, leading to the trachea.

# 4. Muscles
- **Extrinsic:** Move the whole larynx up (swallowing) or down.
- **Intrinsic:** Move the vocal cords (change tension/length).
    - **Cricothyroid:** Tenses/stretches the cords (high pitch).

# 5. Blood Supply
- **Superior Laryngeal Artery:** Branch of the Superior Thyroid Artery.
- **Inferior Laryngeal Artery:** Branch of the Inferior Thyroid Artery.

# 6. Nerve Supply
All laryngeal nerves come from the **Vagus Nerve (CN X)**:
1. **Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve (RLN):** 
    - Motor to **all** intrinsic muscles (except one).
    - Sensory to the larynx **below** the vocal cords.
2. **Superior Laryngeal Nerve (SLN):**
    - **Internal branch:** Sensory to the larynx **above** the vocal cords (the "cough reflex" nerve).
    - **External branch:** Motor to the **Cricothyroid** muscle only.
