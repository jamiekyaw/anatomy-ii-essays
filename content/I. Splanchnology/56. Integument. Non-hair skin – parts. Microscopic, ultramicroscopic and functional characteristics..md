> Integument. Non-hair skin – parts. Microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics.

---

# 1. General Characteristics (The "Parts")
- **Location:** Palms and soles.
- **Distinct Features:** No hair follicles, no sebaceous (oil) glands, higher concentration of sweat glands, and a very thick outer layer.

# 2. Microscopic Structure (The Layers)
The skin is divided into three main sections:
### A. Epidermis (5 Layers - "Come Let’s Get Sun Burned")
1. **Stratum Corneum:** Very thick, dead keratinized cells (protection).
2. **Stratum Lucidum:** **Unique to non-hair skin.** Clear, thin layer of dead cells.
3. **Stratum Granulosum:** Cells contain granules (keratohyalin).
4. **Stratum Spinosum:** "Prickle" layer; provides strength.
5. **Stratum Basale:** Bottom layer; constant cell division (mitosis).
### B. Dermis (Connective Tissue)
- **Papillary Layer:** Contains dermal papillae (creates fingerprints) and **Meissner’s Corpuscles** (fine touch).
- **Reticular Layer:** Deep, dense collagen for strength.
### C. Hypodermis (Subcutis)
- Fatty tissue for insulation and shock absorption.

---

# 3. Ultramicroscopic Characteristics (Cell level)
- **Keratinocytes:** The main cells; filled with keratin protein.
- **Melanocytes:** Produce melanin (pigment) in the basal layer.
- **Desmosomes:** "Buttons" that hold cells together tightly (mechanical strength).
- **Hemidesmosomes:** Anchor the epidermis to the basement membrane.
- **Lamellar Granules (Odland bodies):** Release lipids to make the skin waterproof.

---
# 4. Functional Characteristics
1. **Protection:** Physical barrier against friction, bacteria, and UV light.
2. **Sensation:** High density of receptors for touch (**Meissner’s**) and pressure (**Pacinian corpuscles**).
3. **Thermoregulation:** Loaded with **Eccrine sweat glands** to cool the body.
4. **Grip:** Epidermal ridges (fingerprints) increase friction for holding objects.
5. **Waterproofing:** Prevents water loss from the body.

---
# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Non-hair skin = Thick skin.**
- **Key Layer:** Stratum Lucidum (only in thick skin).
- **Missing parts:** No hair, no oil glands.
- **Sensory:** Meissner corpuscles are the "star" of non-hair skin.
