> Laryngeal cavity. Microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Regions of the Laryngeal Cavity
- **Vestibule:** From the laryngeal inlet to the vestibular folds (false cords).
- **Ventricle:** Small slit-outpouching between false and true cords.
- **Infraglottic space:** From true vocal cords to the lower border of the cricoid cartilage.

---

# 2. Microscopic Characteristics
The larynx is lined by two main types of epithelium depending on mechanical stress:

- **Respiratory Epithelium:** Pseudostratified ciliated columnar with goblet cells.
- **Stratified Squamous Non-keratinized Epithelium:** Found in areas of high friction:
    - Upper half of the Epiglottis.
    - **True Vocal Folds** (Vocal cords).
- **Lamina Propria:** Contains elastic and collagen fibers.
- **Cartilage Framework**

---

# 3. Ultramicroscopic Characteristics (EM Level)
- **Ciliated Cells**
- **Basal Cells**
- **Brush Cells:** Have microvilli; act as sensory receptors (chemoreceptors).
- **Tight Junctions**

---

# 4. Functional Characteristics
1. **Protection:** The cough reflex and closure of the glottis prevent aspiration.
2. **Phonation:** True vocal folds vibrate as air passes through the glottis to produce sound.
3. **Respiration:** The vocal folds abduct (open) to allow air into the trachea.
4. **Effort Closure:** Closing the glottis to increase intra-abdominal pressure (e.g., lifting weights, defecation).

---

# 5. Blood Supply
- **Arterial:**
    1. **Superior Laryngeal Artery:** (Branch of Superior Thyroid Artery). Travels with the Internal Laryngeal Nerve.
    2. **Inferior Laryngeal Artery:** (Branch of Inferior Thyroid Artery). Travels with the Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve.
- **Venous:**
    - **Superior/Inferior Laryngeal Veins:** Drain into Superior and Inferior Thyroid veins, respectively.

---

# 6. Nerve Supply (Crucial for Exams)
All innervation comes from the **Vagus Nerve (CN X)**.

- **Motor (Muscles):**
    - **External Laryngeal Nerve:** Supplies only the **Cricothyroid** muscle (tenses the cords).
    - **Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve (RLN):** Supplies **all other** intrinsic muscles of the larynx.
- **Sensory (Feeling):**
    - **Internal Laryngeal Nerve:** Sensation **above** the vocal folds.
    - **Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve:** Sensation **below** the vocal folds.

---

# Summary Table for Quick Recall
| Feature | True Vocal Fold | Vestibular Fold (False) |

| **Glands** | None (kept moist by others) | Many Seromucous glands |
| **Muscle** | Vocalis muscle present | No muscle |
| **Function** | Sound production | Protection/Lubrication |
