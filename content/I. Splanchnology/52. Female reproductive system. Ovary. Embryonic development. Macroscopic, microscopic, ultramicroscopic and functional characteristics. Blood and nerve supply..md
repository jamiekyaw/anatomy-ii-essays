> Female reproductive system. Ovary. Embryonic development. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:** Derived from the **gonadal ridge** (mesoderm).
- **Germ Cells:** Primordial germ cells migrate from the **yolk sac** wall to the gonadal ridge.
- **Key Fact:** If no Y-chromosome (SRY gene) is present, the ridge automatically develops into an ovary.

# 2. Macroscopic (Gross Anatomy)
- **Shape/Size:** Almond-shaped, paired organs.
- **Location:** Located in the **ovarian fossa** on the lateral pelvic wall.
- **Ligaments (The "Big Three"):**
    1. **Suspensory ligament:** Attaches ovary to pelvic wall (contains ovarian vessels).
    2. **Ovarian ligament:** Attaches ovary to the uterus.
    3. **Mesovarium:** Part of the broad ligament that anchors the ovary.

# 3. Microscopic (Histology)
The ovary is divided into two main zones:
- **Germinal Epithelium:** The outermost layer (simple cuboidal epithelium).
- **Cortex (Outer layer):** Contains **ovarian follicles** in various stages (Primordial → Primary → Secondary → Graafian).
- **Medulla (Inner layer):** Contains loose connective tissue, large blood vessels, and nerves.
- **Corpus Luteum:** What the follicle becomes after ovulation (yellow body); secretes progesterone.

# 4. Ultramicroscopic (Cellular Level)
- **Theca Interna Cells:** Have LH receptors; produce **androstenedione** (androgens).
- **Granulosa Cells:** Have FSH receptors; contain **aromatase** to convert androgens into **estrogen**.
- **Zona Pellucida:** A glycoprotein layer surrounding the oocyte.

# 5. Functional Characteristics
- **Exocrine function:** **Oogenesis** (production and release of the secondary oocyte/egg).
- **Endocrine function:** Production of hormones:
    - **Estrogen:** For secondary sex characteristics and thickening the uterine lining.
    - **Progesterone:** For maintaining pregnancy (secreted by Corpus Luteum).

# 6. Blood and Nerve Supply
- **Arterial Supply:** **Ovarian Artery** (direct branch of the **Abdominal Aorta**).
- **Venous Drainage:**
    - Right Ovarian Vein → **Inferior Vena Cava (IVC)**.
    - Left Ovarian Vein → **Left Renal Vein**. (Common exam question).
- **Nerve Supply:** Ovarian plexus (sympathetic and parasympathetic fibers).

---
**High-Yield Summary for the Examiner:**
*"The ovary develops from the gonadal ridge. It is covered by germinal epithelium, has a cortex containing follicles, and a medulla with vessels. Its main jobs are producing eggs (exocrine) and secreting estrogen and progesterone (endocrine). It is supplied by the ovarian artery from the aorta."*
