> Stomach. Embryonic development. Macroscopic structure of the stomach.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:** Derived from the **distal foregut**.
- **Initial Shape:**
	- A fusiform (spindle-shaped) dilation of the gut tube during the 4th week.
- **Growth:**
	- The dorsal wall grows faster than the ventral wall, creating the **Greater Curvature**.
- **Rotations:**
    1. **90° Clockwise Rotation (Longitudinal axis):** 
        - Left side becomes **Anterior**.
        - Right side becomes **Posterior**.
        - *Result:*
	        - Left Vagus nerve supplies the anterior wall
	        - Right Vagus nerve supplies the posterior wall.
    2. **Anteroposterior Rotation:**
        - The top cardiac part moves left and down.
        - The bottom pyloric part moves right and up.
- **Mesenteries:** 
    - Dorsal mesentery becomes the **Greater Omentum**.
    - Ventral mesentery becomes the **Lesser Omentum**.

# 2. Macroscopic Structure (Anatomy)
The stomach is a J-shaped organ located in the upper left quadrant.

#### A. Main Parts
1. **Cardia:** Where the esophagus enters the stomach.
2. **Fundus:** The dome-shaped top.
3. **Body (Corpus):** The main, central region.
4. **Antrum:** The wider, lower section leading to the exit.
5. **Pylorus:** The narrow canal/sphincter that connects to the duodenum.

#### B. Curvatures
- **Lesser Curvature:**
	- Short, concave right border. Attachment for the Lesser Omentum.
- **Greater Curvature:**
	- Long, convex left border. Attachment for the Greater Omentum.

#### C. Openings/Sphincters
- **Cardiac Orifice:**
	- Lower esophageal sphincter. 
	- Prevents reflux.
- **Pyloric Sphincter:**
	- A true anatomical ring of smooth muscle.
	- Controls stomach emptying.

#### D. Internal Feature
- **Rugae:** Longitudinal mucosal folds. They allow the stomach to expand when food enters. They disappear when the stomach is full.

# 3. Quick Blood Supply
- **Source:** All come from the **Celiac Trunk**.
- **Lesser Curvature:** Left and Right Gastric arteries.
- **Greater Curvature:** Left and Right Gastro-omental (Gastro-epiploic) arteries.
- **Fundus:** Short Gastric arteries.
