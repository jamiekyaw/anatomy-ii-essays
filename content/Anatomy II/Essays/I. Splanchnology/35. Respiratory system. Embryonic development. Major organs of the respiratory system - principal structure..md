# 1. Embryonic Development
The respiratory system begins developing around **Week 4**.

**1. Germ Layers**
-  **Endoderm:** Forms the internal lining of the trachea, bronchi, and lungs.
-  **Splanchnic Mesoderm:** Forms the cartilage, muscle, and connective tissue.

**2. The Lung Bud**
-  Starts as the **Respiratory Diverticulum**.
-  The **Tracheoesophageal septum** separates the trachea from the esophagus.

**3. Stages of Lung Maturation (CRITICAL)**
-  **Pseudoglandular:** No gas exchange possible. Formation of bronchioles.
-  **Canalicular:** Formation of respiratory bronchioles and alveolar ducts. Vascularization increases.
-  **Saccular:** Primitive alveoli form. **Surfactant** begins to be produced.
-  **Alveolar (Birth–8 years):** Mature alveoli form.

# 2. Major Organs & Structure
The system is divided into the **Conducting Zone** (moves air) and the **Respiratory Zone** (gas exchange).

## 1. The Larynx (Voice Box)
-  **Structure:** Made of 9 cartilages (Thyroid, Cricoid, Epiglottis are the main ones).
-  **Function:** Houses vocal cords; the **Epiglottis** prevents food from entering the trachea.

## 2. The Trachea (Windpipe)
-  **Structure:** Flexible tube held open by **C-shaped hyaline cartilage rings**.
-  **Lining:** Pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium.

## 3. The Bronchial Tree
-  **Trachea $\rightarrow$ Primary Bronchi $\rightarrow$ Secondary (Lobar) $\rightarrow$ Tertiary (Segmental) $\rightarrow$ Bronchioles.**
-  **Structural change:** As you go deeper, cartilage decreases and **smooth muscle increases**.
-  **Bronchioles:** Have *no* cartilage. They can constrict (Asthma).

## 4. The Alveoli
-  **Structure:** Tiny air sacs where gas exchange occurs.
-  **Cell Types:**
    -  **Type I Pneumocytes:** Thin, flat cells for gas diffusion.
    -  **Type II Pneumocytes:** Cuboidal cells that secrete **Surfactant**.
    -  **Alveolar Macrophages:** "Dust cells" that clean up debris.

## 5. The Lungs & Pleura
-  **Right Lung:** 3 lobes (Superior, Middle, Inferior).
-  **Left Lung:** 2 lobes (Superior, Inferior) + Cardiac Notch (space for heart).
-  **Pleura:** Double-layered membrane.
    -  *Visceral pleura:* Touches the lung.
    -  *Parietal pleura:* Touches the chest wall.
    -  *Pleural Cavity:* Space between layers with fluid to reduce friction.
