# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:**
	- Derived from the **foregut** (endoderm).
- **Timing:**
	- Appears in the 4th week.
- **Hepatic Diverticulum:**
	- An outgrowth from the duodenum that grows into the **septum transversum**.
- **Division of the Bud:**
    1. **Cranial part:** Becomes the liver parenchyma (hepatocytes).
    2. **Caudal part:** Becomes the gallbladder and cystic duct.
- **Tissue Origins:**
    - **Endoderm:* Hepatocytes and lining of the bile ducts.
    - **Mesoderm:**
	    - Kupffer cells, hematopoietic cells, and connective tissue (Glisson’s capsule).
- **Vascular changes:**
	- In utero, the **left umbilical vein** brings oxygenated blood to the liver
	- After birth, it fibroses into the **Ligamentum Teres**.

# 2. Macroscopic Structure

## 1. Location & General Features
Largest gland in the body
- **Position:**
	- Right hypochondrium and epigastrium
	- Tucked under the diaphragm
- **Surfaces:**
    1. **Diaphragmatic:** Smooth, dome-shaped (superior/anterior).
    2. **Visceral:** Irregular, faces organs (inferior/posterior).

## 2. Lobes
Divided by the **Falciform ligament** and sagittal fissures:
1. **Right Lobe:** Largest.
2. **Left Lobe:** Smaller, flattened.
3. **Quadrate Lobe:** On the inferior surface (between gallbladder and round ligament).
4. **Caudate Lobe:** On the posterior surface (between IVC and ligamentum venosum).

## 3. Porta Hepatis
A deep fissure on the visceral surface where structures enter/exit.
Contains the **Portal Triad**:
1. **Hepatic Portal Vein** - brings nutrient-rich blood.
2. **Hepatic Artery Proper** - brings oxygenated blood.
3. **Common Hepatic Duct** - drains bile.

## 4. Ligaments & Folds
- **Falciform Ligament:**
	- Attaches liver to the anterior abdominal wall.
- **Round Ligament:**
	- Remnant of the umbilical vein; found in the free edge of the falciform.
- **Ligamentum Venosum:**
	- Remnant of the ductus venosus.
- **Coronary Ligaments:**
	- Reflect onto the diaphragm, outlining the **"Bare Area"** (part of the liver not covered by peritoneum).

## 5. Visceral Impressions
The liver touches and is indented by:
- **Left**
	- Stomach
- **Right**
	- Right Kidney
- **Near gallbladder**
	- Duodenum
- **Inferior**
	- Right Colic Flexure
