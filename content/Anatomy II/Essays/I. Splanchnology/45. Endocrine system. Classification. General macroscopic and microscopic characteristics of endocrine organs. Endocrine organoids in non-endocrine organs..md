> Endocrine system. Classification. General macroscopic and microscopic characteristics of endocrine organs. Endocrine organoids in non-endocrine organs.

---
# 1. General Concept
- **Definition:** A system of ductless glands that secrete **hormones** directly into the **bloodstream** to regulate distant target organs.
- **Function:** Maintain homeostasis, growth, metabolism, and reproduction.

---

# 2. Classification of Endocrine Organs
1. **Central Organs (The "Command Center"):**
    - Hypothalamus (links nervous and endocrine systems).
    - Pituitary Gland (Hypophysis).
    - Pineal Gland (Epiphysis).
2. **Peripheral Organs (Pure Endocrine):**
    - Thyroid Gland.
    - Parathyroid Glands.
    - Adrenal Glands (Suprarenal).
3. **Mixed Organs (Endocrine + Exocrine functions):**
    - Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans).
    - Gonads (Testes and Ovaries).
    - Kidneys and Thymus.
4. **Diffuse Endocrine System (DNES / APUD cells):**
    - Single cells scattered in the respiratory and GI tracts.

# 3. General Macroscopic Characteristics
- **Ductless:** They do not have tubes; they dump secretions directly into capillaries.
- **Rich Blood Supply:** They are the most highly vascularized organs in the body (needed to transport hormones).
- **Small Size:** Usually small, but have a massive physiological effect.

---

# 4. General Microscopic Characteristics
Every endocrine gland follows this basic histological blueprint:
- **Stroma:** A connective tissue capsule that sends septa (walls) into the gland to carry blood vessels and nerves.
- **Parenchyma (Functional cells):** Usually arranged in two ways:
    1. **Cords:** Cells lined up in rows (e.g., Adrenal cortex, Pituitary).
    2. **Follicles:** Cells forming a hollow sphere (Unique to the **Thyroid**).
- **Capillaries:** Specifically **Fenestrated Capillaries** (they have "pores" or "windows" to allow large hormone molecules to enter the blood quickly).
- **Organelles:**
    - Protein-secreting cells: Rich in **Rough ER**.
    - Steroid-secreting cells: Rich in **Smooth ER** and lipid droplets.

---

# 5. Endocrine Organoids in Non-Endocrine Organs
These are clusters of endocrine cells (organoids) embedded within organs that have other primary jobs:

1. **Pancreas:** *Islets of Langerhans* (Produce Insulin/Glucagon).
2. **Testes:** *Leydig Cells* (Produce Testosterone).
3. **Ovaries:** *Theca Interna and Corpus Luteum* (Produce Estrogen/Progesterone).
4. **Kidney:** *Juxtaglomerular cells* (Produce Renin).
5. **Heart:** Atrial myocytes (Produce ANP to lower blood pressure).
6. **Gastrointestinal Tract (APUD System):** Enteroendocrine cells (Gastrin, Secretin, Cholecystokinin). *Note: The GI tract is technically the largest endocrine organ in the body.*

---

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Ductless?** Yes.
- **Vascular?** Extremely.
- **Capillaries?** Fenestrated.
- **Arrangement?** Cords or Follicles.
- **Mixed Organs?** Pancreas and Gonads.
