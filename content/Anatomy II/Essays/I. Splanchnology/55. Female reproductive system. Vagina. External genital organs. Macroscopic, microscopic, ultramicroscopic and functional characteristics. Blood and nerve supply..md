# 1. THE VAGINA

![|300](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/22469-vagina)

**Vagina Macroscopic**
- **Definition:** A muscular tube (approx. 7–10 cm long) connecting the cervix to the outside.
- **Location:** Between the bladder and rectum.
- **Fornices:**
	- The upper end surrounds the cervix, creating pockets called the **anterior, posterior, and lateral fornices**.
	- The posterior fornix is the deepest.

**Vagina Microscopic**
- **Mucosa:** Lined with **Stratified Squamous Non-Keratinized Epithelium**. 
- **Muscularis:** Smooth muscle layers (inner circular, outer longitudinal).
- **Adventitia:** Outer layer of dense connective tissue.

**Vagina Ultramicroscopic**
- **Glycogen:** Epithelial cells are rich in glycogen. 
- **pH:** Bacteria (*Lactobacillus*) break down glycogen into **lactic acid**, keeping the vagina acidic (pH 3.5–4.5) to prevent infection.

**Vagina Functional Characteristics**
- Organ of copulation (intercourse).
- Birth canal during parturition.
- Exit route for menstrual blood.

**Vagina Blood and Nerve Supply**
- **Arteries:** Vaginal artery and Uterine artery (branches of **Internal Iliac artery**).
- **Nerves:** 
    - Lower 1/4: **Pudendal nerve** (sensitive to pain/touch).
    - Upper 3/4: **Uterovaginal plexus** (autonomic; less sensitive to pain).

# 2. EXTERNAL GENITAL ORGANS (VULVA)

**External Genital Organs Macroscopic Components**
1. **Mons Pubis:** Fatty tissue pad over the pubic bone (covered in hair).
2. **Labia Majora:** Large outer folds of skin (contain fat and sweat glands).
3. **Labia Minora:** Small inner folds (no hair, no fat).
4. **Clitoris:** Small erectile body; highly sensitive.
5. **Vestibule:** The space between labia minora; contains the urethral opening and vaginal opening.
6. **Bartholin’s Glands (Greater Vestibular):** Located on either side of the vaginal opening.

**External Genital Organs Microscopic Characteristics**
- **Labia Majora:** Keratinized stratified squamous epithelium with hair follicles.
- **Labia Minora:** Core of vascular connective tissue (no hair).
- **Clitoris:** Contains **Corpora Cavernosa** (erectile tissue that fills with blood).

**External Genital Organs Functional Characteristics**
- **Protection:** Covers the internal reproductive tract.
- **Lubrication:** Bartholin’s glands secrete mucus during arousal.
- **Sensory:** Clitoris is the primary site of sexual stimulation.

**External Genital Organs Blood and Nerve Supply**
- **Arteries:** **Internal Pudendal artery**.
- **Nerves:** **Pudendal nerve** (S2-S4).

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Vagina Epithelium:** Stratified squamous non-keratinized.
- **Vagina Glands:** None (important!).
- **Vagina pH:** Acidic (due to Lactobacilli + Glycogen).
- **Main Artery:** Internal Iliac / Internal Pudendal.
- **Main Nerve:** Pudendal Nerve.
- **External Gland:** Bartholin’s Gland (for lubrication).
