> Endocrine system. Suprarenal gland. Embryonic development. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply. APUD – system.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development (Dual Origin)
- **Adrenal Cortex:** Derived from **Mesoderm**.
- **Adrenal Medulla:** Derived from **Ectoderm**.
- *Clinical Note:* This explains why the two parts function as two completely different organs.

# 2. Macroscopic (Gross Anatomy)
- **Location:** Retroperitoneal, superior pole of each kidney (T12 level).
- **Shape:** 
    - **Right:** Pyramidal (triangular).
    - **Left:** Semilunar shaped and usually larger.
- **Structure:** Outer yellow cortex (firm) and inner dark-red medulla (soft).

### 3. Microscopic (Histology)
The cortex is divided into three zones (**Mnemonic: GFR**):
1. **Zona Glomerulosa (Outer):**
	1. Cells arranged in inverted U-shaped clusters or "arches."
2. **Zona Fasciculata (Middle/Thickest):**
	1. Cells arranged in long, straight parallel cords.
	2. Cells are called *spongiocytes* because they look bubbly (lipid droplets).
3. **Zona Reticularis (Inner):**
	1. Cells arranged in a branching, irregular network.

**Medulla:**
- Contains **Chromaffin cells** (modified postganglionic sympathetic neurons).
- Large venous sinusoids.

# 4. Ultramicroscopic (Cellular Level)
- **Cortex Cells (Steroid-producing):**
    - Abundant **Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER)**.
    - Mitochondria with **tubular cristae**.
    - Numerous lipid droplets (cholesterol storage).
- **Medulla Cells (Protein/Amine-producing):**
    - Abundant **Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER)** and Golgi.
    - **Dense-core granules** (containing adrenaline/noradrenaline).

# 5. Functional Characteristics (Hormones)
**Mnemonic: Salt, Sugar, Sex (the deeper you go, the sweeter it gets)**
- **Glomerulosa:** Mineralocorticoids (**Aldosterone**)
	- Controls Na+/K+ balance.
- **Fasciculata:** Glucocorticoids (**Cortisol**)
	- Glucose metabolism and stress response.
- **Reticularis:** Gonadocorticoids (**Androgens/DHEA**)
	- Sex hormones.
- **Medulla:** Catecholamines (**Adrenaline & Noradrenaline**)
	- "Fight or Flight" response.

# 6. Blood Supply & Nerve Supply
- **Arterial Supply:**
    1. Superior Suprarenal (from Inferior Phrenic artery).
    2. Middle Suprarenal (directly from Abdominal Aorta).
    3. Inferior Suprarenal (from Renal artery).
- **Venous Drainage:**
    - **Right** vein drains directly into the **Inferior Vena Cava (IVC)**.
    - **Left** vein drains into the **Left Renal Vein**.
- **Nerve Supply:**
    - Mainly **Preganglionic Sympathetic Fibers** (Splanchic nerves). 

# 7. APUD System
- **Stands for:**
	- **A**mine **P**recursor **U**ptake and **D**ecarboxylation.
- **Definition:**
	- A group of endocrine cells that share the ability to produce biologically active amines and peptides.
- **In Adrenals:**
	- The **Chromaffin cells** of the medulla are a major component of the APUD system.
- **Significance:** They are derived from the neural crest and link the endocrine system with the nervous system (Neuroendocrine).
