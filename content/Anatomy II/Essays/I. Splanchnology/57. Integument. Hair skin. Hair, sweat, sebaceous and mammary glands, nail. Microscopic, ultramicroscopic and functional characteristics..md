> Integument. Hair skin. Hair, sweat, sebaceous and mammary glands, nail. Microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics.

---

# 1. The Skin (Two Main Layers)
- **Epidermis (Outer Layer):** Avascular (no blood vessels), stratified squamous keratinized epithelium.
    - **5 Layers (Bottom to Top):**
	    * Basale
	    * Spinosum
	    * Granulosum
	    * Lucidum
	    * Corneum (dead cells/keratin).
    - **Cells:**
	    - Keratinocytes (strength)
	    - Melanocytes (pigment/UV protection)
	    - Langerhans (immune defense).
- **Dermis (Inner Layer):** Vascular connective tissue.
    - Contains collagen (strength) and elastin (stretch).
    - **Papillary Layer:** Top layer, creates fingerprints.
    - **Reticular Layer:** Deep layer, contains glands and hair follicles.

# 2. Hair
- **Structure:**
	- Hair shaft (visible)
	- Hair root (under skin)
	- Hair bulb (base where growth happens).
- **Arrector Pili Muscle:** Small smooth muscle attached to the follicle; causes "goosebumps" to trap heat.
- **Function:** Thermoregulation and sensation.

# 3. Sebaceous (Oil) Glands

**Sebaceous (Oil) Glands:**
- **Location:** Usually attached to hair follicles.
- **Secretory Mode:** **Holocrine** (the whole cell ruptures to release secretion).
- **Product:** Sebum (oil) – lubricates skin and kills bacteria.

# 4. Sweat Glands

**Sweat glands:**
- **Eccrine Glands:** Found everywhere. Produce watery sweat for **thermoregulation**.
- **Apocrine Glands:** Found in armpits/groin. Produce thicker, smelly sweat. Become active at puberty.

# 5. Mammary Glands

**Mammary Glands:**
- **Type:** Modified apocrine sweat glands.
- **Structure:** Composed of lobes and **alveoli** (milk-producing sacs).
- **Function:** Synthesis and ejection of milk (regulated by hormones Prolactin and Oxytocin).

# 6. Nails
- **Structure:** Nail plate (hard part), Nail bed (skin under plate), Matrix (where growth occurs).
- **Composition:** Formed by "Hard Keratin" which does not desquamate (shed).

# 7. Microscopic / Functional Summary (Ultra-High Yield)
- **Ultramicroscopic Point:**
	- **Desmosomes** hold skin cells together for mechanical strength. 
	- **Melanosomes** transfer pigment from melanocytes to keratinocytes.
- **Primary Functions:**
    1. **Protection:** Barrier against UV, water loss, and microbes.
    2. **Thermoregulation:** Through sweat and blood flow adjustment.
    3. **Sensation:** Receptors for touch, pain, and temperature.
    4. **Synthesis:** Produces Vitamin D via UV light.
