# 1. Embryonic Development of the Heart

The heart is the first functional organ to develop, starting around **week 3**.

### The Heart Tube
The heart develops from **mesoderm**. It starts as two tubes that fuse into a single **primitive heart tube**. From top to bottom (cranial to caudal), the tube has five regions:
1. **Truncus Arteriosus:** Becomes the Ascending Aorta and Pulmonary Trunk.
2. **Bulbus Cordis:** Becomes the Right Ventricle and the outflow tracts.
3. **Primitive Ventricle:** Becomes the Left Ventricle.
4. **Primitive Atrium:** Becomes the trabeculated parts of the Left and Right Atria.
5. **Sinus Venosus:** Becomes the smooth part of the Right Atrium (Sinus Venarum), the SA node, and the Coronary Sinus.

![|400](https://www.utmb.edu/pedi_ed/CoreV2/CardiologyPart1/The%20heart%20tube.jpg)

### Cardiac Looping
Around day 23-28, the heart tube grows faster than the space around it, forcing it to fold into an **"S-shape."** This brings the atria (originally at the bottom) to sit above the ventricles.

![](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/images/thumb/4/4b/Heart_Looping_Sequence.jpg/1050px-Heart_Looping_Sequence.jpg)

### Septation (Dividing the Chambers)
- **Atrial Septum:** Formed by the *Septum Primum* and *Septum Secundum*. The gap between them is the **Foramen Ovale**, which allows blood to bypass the lungs in the fetus.
- **Ventricular Septum:** A muscular wall grows upward, meeting a thin membranous part from the top.

# 2. Macroscopic (External) Characteristics

### Location and Layers
- **Location:** In the **middle mediastinum**, protected by the sternum and ribs.
- **Apex:** Formed by the **Left Ventricle**; points down and to the left (5th intercostal space).
- **Base:** Formed mainly by the **Left Atrium**; faces posteriorly.
- **Layers:** 
    1. *Epicardium* (Outer protective layer/visceral pericardium).
    2. *Myocardium* (Middle muscular layer).
    3. *Endocardium* (Inner lining).

![|400](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/Diagram_of_the_human_heart_%28cropped%29.svg/3840px-Diagram_of_the_human_heart_%28cropped%29.svg.png)

### Surface Grooves (Sulci)
- **Coronary Sulcus:** Circles the heart, separating atria from ventricles. Contains coronary arteries.
- **Anterior & Posterior Interventricular Sulci:** Marks the boundary between the left and right ventricles.

# 3. The Atria (Receiving Chambers)

### Right Atrium (RA) - Deoxygenated Blood
- **Inputs:** Superior Vena Cava (SVC), Inferior Vena Cava (IVC), and Coronary Sinus.
- **Right Auricle:** A small, cone-shaped muscular pouch (increases capacity).
- **Fossa Ovalis:** An oval depression in the septum; the remnant of the fetal Foramen Ovale.
- **Pectinate Muscles:** Rough muscular ridges on the interior wall.
- **Crista Terminalis:** The internal ridge separating the smooth and rough parts of the atrium.

![|400](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/335104247/figure/fig1/AS:871954122817537@1584901471256/Anatomical-features-of-the-right-atrium-Illustration-by-Katie-Yost-MS-CMI-C2019.jpg)

### Left Atrium (LA) - Oxygenated Blood
- **Inputs:** Four **Pulmonary Veins**.
- The wall is slightly thicker than the Right Atrium.
- Mostly smooth-walled; pectinate muscles are only found in the Left Auricle.

# 4. The Ventricles (Pumping Chambers)

### Right Ventricle (RV)
- **Function:** Pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs via the **Pulmonary Trunk**.
- **Tricuspid Valve:** Separates RA from RV.
- **Trabeculae Carneae:** Irregular muscular ridges (prevent suction).
- **Papillary Muscles & Chordae Tendineae:** The "heart strings" that prevent the valve leaflets from everting (turning inside out) during contraction.
- **Conus Arteriosus (Infundibulum):** The smooth outflow tract leading to the pulmonary valve.

![|400](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/336036268/figure/fig2/AS:864745875841025@1583182891281/Anatomical-features-of-the-right-ventricle-demonstrating-the-internal-structures.jpg)

![|400](https://www.vhlab.umn.edu/atlas/comparative-anatomy-tutorial/graphics/label-Heart0144-Perfusion-Fixed-Right-Ventricle-Posterior-Papillary-Muscle.jpg)

### Left Ventricle (LV)
- **Function:** Pumps oxygenated blood to the whole body via the **Aorta**.
- **Thickness:** The wall of the LV is **3x thicker** than the RV because it must pump against high systemic pressure.
- **Mitral (Bicuspid) Valve:** Separates LA from LV.
- **Aortic Vestibule:** The smooth outflow tract leading to the aortic valve.

# Summary Table for Quick Revision

| Feature | Right Side | Left Side |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
