> Submandibular and sublingual gland. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Submandibular Gland

### Macroscopic
- **Size:** Second largest salivary gland (size of a walnut).
- **Location:** Occupies the submandibular triangle.
- **Duct:** **Wharton’s Duct**.

### Microscopic:
- **Type:** Compound tubuloalveolar gland.
- **Composition:** **Mixed** but **Predominantly Serous**.
- **Feature:** Contains **Serous Demilunes** (crescent-shaped serous cells capping mucous tubules).

### Ultramicroscopic (EM)
- **Serous cells:** Abundant Rough ER, Golgi apparatus, and apical **Zymogen granules** (storing enzymes).
- **Myoepithelial cells** (contract to expel saliva).
- **Function:** Produces 60–70% of total daily saliva volume.

# 2. Sublingual Gland
### Macroscopic
- **Size:** Smallest of the three major salivary glands. Almond-shaped.
- **Location:** Floor of the mouth.
- **Ducts:** No single main duct. 8–20 small ducts (**Ducts of Rivinus**). The largest is the **Bartholin’s duct** (often joins Wharton’s duct).

### Microscopic
- **Type:** Compound tubuloalveolar gland.
- **Composition:** **Mixed** but **Predominantly Mucous**.
- **Duct system:** Striated and intercalated ducts are very short or absent compared to other glands.

### Ultramicroscopic (EM)
- **Mucous cells:** Filled with large, pale **mucinogen droplets** that compress the nucleus against the basal membrane.
- **Function:** Produces 3–5% of total saliva (mostly for lubrication).

# 3. Blood Supply & Lymphatics
- **Arterial:** 
    - Submandibular: **Facial** and **Lingual** arteries.
    - Sublingual: **Sublingual** and **Submental** arteries.
- **Venous:** Drain into the Common Facial and Lingual veins.
- **Lymphatics:** Both drain into **Submandibular lymph nodes**, then into Deep Cervical nodes.

---

# 4. Nerve Supply (Crucial for Exams)
The pathway is nearly identical for both glands:

1. **Parasympathetic (Secretomotor/Stimulatory):**
    - **Origin:** Superior Salivatory Nucleus (Pons).
    - **Nerve:** **CN VII (Facial)** $\rightarrow$ Chorda Tympani $\rightarrow$ joins **Lingual Nerve** (branch of CN V3).
2. **Sympathetic (Vasoconstrictive):**
    - **Origin:** T1–T3 spinal segments.
    - **Effect:** Produces thick, protein-rich saliva (dry mouth feeling).
3. **Sensory:** Lingual nerve (branch of Mandibular nerve V3).
