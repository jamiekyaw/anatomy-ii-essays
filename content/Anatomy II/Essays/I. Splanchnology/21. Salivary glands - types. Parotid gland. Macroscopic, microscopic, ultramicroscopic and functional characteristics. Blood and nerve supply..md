> Salivary glands - types. Parotid gland. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Types of Salivary Glands
- **Major (3 pairs):**
    1. **Parotid:** Largest; Purely **Serous** (watery).
    2. **Submandibular:** **Mixed** (mostly serous).
    3. **Sublingual:** **Mixed** (mostly mucous).
- **Minor:** Hundreds of small glands in the oral mucosa (e.g., labial, buccal, palatine).

---

# 2. Parotid Gland: Macroscopic (Gross)
- **Location:** Inverted triangle shape, located in front of and below the ear.
- **Duct:** **Stensen’s Duct**. It opens into the oral cavity opposite the **upper 2nd molar**.
- **Key Structures passing through the gland:**
    1. **Facial Nerve (CN VII):** Divides into 5 branches (does *not* supply the gland, just passes through).
    2. **Retromandibular Vein.**
    3. **External Carotid Artery.**

# 3. Microscopic Characteristics (Histology)
- **Type:** Compound tubuloalveolar gland.
- **Acinus (Secretory unit):** Purely **Serous**.
    - Cells are pyramidal with a central, round nucleus.
    - Cytoplasm is basophilic (purple/blue) due to protein-making machinery.
- **Duct System:**
    - **Intercalated ducts:** Low cuboidal cells.
    - **Striated ducts:** Main site for electrolyte reabsorption (looks "striated" due to mitochondria).

# 4. Ultramicroscopic (Electron Microscope)
- **Basal region:**
	- Packed with **Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER)** for protein synthesis.
- **Golgi apparatus:**
	- Well-developed for packaging enzymes.
- **Apical region:**
	- Filled with **Zymogen granules** (storing alpha-amylase).
- **Myoepithelial cells:**
	- Contractile cells surrounding the acini to "squeeze" saliva out.

---

# 5. Functional Characteristics
- **Digestion:** Secretes **Alpha-amylase (Ptyalin)** to break down cooked starch.
- **Protection:** Secretes IgA (antibodies) and Lysozyme (antibacterial).
- **Lubrication:** Keeps mouth moist for speech and swallowing.

---

# 6. Blood Supply
- **Arterial:**
	- External Carotid Artery and its terminal branches
	- (Superficial Temporal and Maxillary arteries).
- **Venous:**
	- Drains into the **Retromandibular Vein**.

---

# 7. Nerve Supply (Crucial for Exams)
- **Parasympathetic (Secretomotor - triggers saliva):**
    - Origin: Inferior Salivatory Nucleus
    - $\rightarrow$ **Glossopharyngeal Nerve (CN IX)**
    - $\rightarrow$ Lesser Petrosal Nerve
    - $\rightarrow$**Otic Ganglion** (Synapse)
    - $\rightarrow$ Auriculotemporal Nerve
    - $\rightarrow$ Gland.
- **Sympathetic:**
	- From the plexus around the External Carotid Artery (causes vasoconstriction/thick saliva).
- **Sensory:** Auriculotemporal Nerve and Great Auricular Nerve (C2, C3).
