> Male reproductive system. Testis and epididymis. Embryonic development. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood-testicular barrier. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:** Develop from the **genital ridges** (mesoderm).
- **Key Gene:** **SRY gene** on the Y chromosome triggers male differentiation.
- **Descent:** Testes descend from the abdomen into the scrotum through the **inguinal canal**, guided by a ligament called the **gubernaculum**.
- **Temperature:** Must be $2\text{--}3^\circ\text{C}$ cooler than body temperature for sperm production.

# 2. Macroscopic Anatomy (Gross Anatomy)
- **Tunica Albuginea:** A tough, white fibrous capsule surrounding the testis.
- **Septa:** Extensions of the capsule that divide the testis into ~250 **lobules**.
- **Lobules:** Each contains 1–4 **seminiferous tubules** (where sperm are made).
- **Rete Testis:** A network of canals at the hilum where sperm collect before exiting.
- **Epididymis:** A coiled tube sitting on the back of the testis. Divided into **Head, Body, and Tail**.

# 3. Microscopic & Ultramicroscopic Anatomy
There are three main cell types you must name:

**A. Spermatogenic Cells (Inside Tubules)**
- The layers of developing sperm: *Spermatogonia* (stem cells) $\rightarrow$ *Spermatocytes* $\rightarrow$ *Spermatids* $\rightarrow$ *Spermatozoa*.

**B. Sertoli Cells ("The Nurses" - Inside Tubules)**
- **Function:** Support, protect, and nourish developing sperm.
- **Ultrastructure:** Form **Tight Junctions** with each other.
- **Secretions:** Produce **ABP** (Androgen Binding Protein) and **Inhibin**.

**C. Leydig Cells (In the Interstitial Space *between* tubules)**
- **Function:** Produce **Testosterone**.
- **Ultrastructure:** Rich in **Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER)** and mitochondria (typical for steroid-producing cells).

# 4. The Blood-Testis Barrier (BTB)
- **Location:** Formed by **Tight Junctions** between adjacent **Sertoli cells**.
- **Function:** 
    1. Prevents the immune system from attacking "foreign" developing sperm (which are haploid).
    2. Creates a specific chemical environment for sperm to mature.
- **Divides the tubule into:** Basal compartment (bottom) and Adluminal compartment (top).

# 5. The Epididymis
- **Histology:** Lined with **pseudostratified columnar epithelium** with **stereocilia** (long, non-motile microvilli).
- **Function:** 
    1. **Sperm Maturation:** Sperm gain motility here.
    2. **Storage:** Sperm are stored in the **tail** until ejaculation.
    3. **Absorption:** Reabsorbs fluid coming from the testes.

# 6. Blood and Nerve Supply
- **Arterial:** **Testicular Artery** (branch of the Abdominal Aorta).
- **Venous:** **Pampiniform Plexus** (a network of veins).
    - *Function:* Acts as a heat exchanger to cool the arterial blood before it reaches the testis.
    - Drains into the Testicular Vein.
- **Nerves:** Autonomic fibers from the **T10** spinal level.

1. **Sertoli cells** = Support / Blood-Testis Barrier.
2. **Leydig cells** = Testosterone (Found *outside* the tubules).
3. **Pampiniform Plexus** = Cooling system.
4. **Stereocilia** = Found in the Epididymis.
5. **SRY gene** = Makes the embryo male.
