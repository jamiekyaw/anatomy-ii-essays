> Lungs. Embryonic development. Macroscopic structure. Bronchial tree - microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:**
	- Starts at **Week 4** from the **Respiratory Diverticulum**
	- An outgrowth of the **foregut endoderm**.
- **Stages of Maturation:**
    - **Pseudoglandular (6-16 weeks):**
	    - Branching continues; no gas exchange possible.
    - **Canalicular (16-26 weeks):**
	    - Lumens enlarge; vascularization increases.
	    - **Surfactant** starts being produced at the end.
    - **Saccular (26 weeks-Birth):**
	    - Primitive alveoli form; blood-air barrier established. (Survival is possible).
    - **Alveolar (8 months-8 years):**
	    - Mature alveoli form
	    - 95% of alveoli develop *after* birth

---

# 2. Macroscopic Structure
- **General:**
	- Located in the thoracic cavity, surrounded by **pleura** (visceral and parietal).
- **Right Lung:**
    - 3 Lobes (Superior, Middle, Inferior).
    - 2 Fissures (Horizontal and Oblique).
    - Shorter/wider (due to liver).
- **Left Lung:**
    - 2 Lobes (Superior, Inferior).
    - 1 Fissure (Oblique).
    - Features:
	    - **Cardiac Notch**
	    - **Lingula**.
- **Hilum (The Root):** Where structures enter/exit. Contains: 
    - Main bronchus
    - Pulmonary artery (superior)
    - 2 Pulmonary veins (inferior)
    - Nerves
    - Lymphatics.

---

# 3. The Bronchial Tree (Pathways)
- **Conducting Zone (No gas exchange):**
	- Trachea $\rightarrow$ Main Bronchi $\rightarrow$ Lobar Bronchi $\rightarrow$ Segmental Bronchi $\rightarrow$ Bronchioles $\rightarrow$ Terminal Bronchioles.
- **Respiratory Zone (Gas exchange):**
	- Respiratory Bronchioles $\rightarrow$ Alveolar Ducts $\rightarrow$ Alveolar Sacs $\rightarrow$ Alveoli.

---

### 4. Microscopic & Ultramicroscopic Characteristics

| Structure       | Epithelium Type                    | Support Structures                                             |
| :-------------- | :--------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bronchi**     | Ciliated Pseudostratified Columnar | Cartilage **plates**, smooth muscle, goblet cells.             |
| **Bronchioles** | Ciliated Simple Columnar/Cuboidal  | **No cartilage**, thick smooth muscle, **Clara (Club) cells**. |
| **Alveoli**     | Simple Squamous                    | Elastic fibers, Capillaries.                                   |

- **Key Cells:**
    - **Type I Pneumocytes (95% surface):**
	    - Simple squamous
	    - Ultra-thin for gas exchange
    - **Type II Pneumocytes (5% surface):**
	    - Cuboidal
	    - Produce **Surfactant** (prevents alveolar collapse).
    - **Alveolar Macrophages:**
	    - Clean up debris/bacteria.
    - **Club (Clara) Cells:**
	    - In bronchioles
	    - Secrete protective liquid and act as stem cells.

---

### 5. Functional Characteristics
- **Gas Exchange:**
	- Occurs across the **Blood-Air Barrier**.
	    - *Components:* Type I Pneumocyte $\rightarrow$ Fused Basement Membrane $\rightarrow$ Capillary Endothelial cell.
- **Conditioning:**
	- The conducting zone warms
	- Filters, and moistens air via mucus and cilia (**Mucociliary Escalator**).
- **Compliance:**
	- Ability of lungs to stretch (elastic fibers).
- **Surface Tension:**
	- Reduced by Surfactant to keep small airways open.
