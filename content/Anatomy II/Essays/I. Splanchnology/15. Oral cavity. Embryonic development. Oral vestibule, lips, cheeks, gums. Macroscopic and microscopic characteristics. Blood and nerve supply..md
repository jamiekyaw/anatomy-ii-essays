> Oral cavity. Embryonic development. Oral vestibule, lips, cheeks, gums. Macroscopic and microscopic characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **The Stomodeum:**
	- The primitive mouth.
	- It is an invagination of **ectoderm** separated from the foregut by the *oropharyngeal membrane* (which ruptures at week 4).
-   **Pharyngeal Arches:**
    - **1st Arch (Mandibular):** Gives rise to the mandible, maxilla, and the muscles of mastication.
    - **Lips:** Formed by the fusion of the maxillary processes and the medial nasal processes.
    - **Cheeks:** Formed by the fusion of the maxillary and mandibular prominences.
- **Palate:**
	- Formed by the primary palate (from medial nasal processes) and the secondary palate (from maxillary processes).
- **Tongue:**
	- Develops from Arches 1, 2, 3, and 4.

# 2. Oral Vestibule
The oral cavity is divided into the **Vestibule** (outer) and **Oral Cavity Proper** (inner).
- **Boundaries:**
    * External: Lips and cheeks.
    * Internal: Teeth and gums (gingivae).
- **Communication:**
	- Communicates with the exterior via the *rima oris* (mouth opening) and with the oral cavity proper behind the 3rd molars.
- **Clinical Landmark:**
	- The **Parotid Duct (Stensen’s duct)** opens into the vestibule opposite the **upper 2nd molar**.

# 3. Lips (Labia Oris)
## Macroscopic
- **Philtrum:** The vertical groove in the center of the upper lip.
- **Vermillion Border:** The "red area" of the lip (transition zone).
- **Labial Frenulum:** Midline folds of mucous membrane connecting lips to gums.

![|400](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/lip-anatomy.jpg)

## Microscopic (The 3 Parts)
1. **Cutaneous (Skin) Side:**
	- Keratinized stratified squamous epithelium with hair follicles, sebaceous glands, and sweat glands.
2. **Vermillion Zone (Red Margin):** 
    - Modified skin. Very thin keratinized epithelium.
    - **No glands/hair.** 
    - High connective tissue papillae with rich blood supply (gives the red color).
3. **Oral Mucosa Side:**
	- Non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium.
	- Contains **Labial glands** (seromucous) in the submucosa.
4. **Core:**
	- Contains the **Orbicularis oris** muscle (skeletal muscle).

# 4. Cheeks (Buccae)
## Macroscopic
- Forms the lateral walls of the oral cavity.
- Bounded by the buccinator muscle.

## Microscopic
1. **Mucosa:** Non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium.
2. **Submucosa:** Contains elastic fibers and **Buccal glands** (minor salivary glands).
3. **Muscularis:** The **Buccinator muscle**.
4. **Fat:** The **Buccal fat pad** (of Bichat) sits between the buccinator and masseter muscles (prominent in infants for sucking).

---

# 5. Gums (Gingivae)
## Macroscopic
- Soft tissue covering the alveolar ridges of the maxilla and mandible.
- **Free Gingiva:** The edge surrounding the tooth.
- **Attached Gingiva:** Firmly bound to the bone.
- **Gingival Sulcus:** The small "pocket" between the tooth and the free gingiva.


## Microscopic
- **Epithelium:**
	- Stratified squamous **keratinized** or parakeratinized (to withstand the friction of chewing).
- **Lamina Propria:**
	- Thick, dense connective tissue with long papillae.
- **Note:**
	- The gums have **no submucosa**.
	- The lamina propria attaches directly to the periosteum of the bone (**Mucoperiosteum**).

# 6. Blood and Nerve Supply

| Structure | Blood Supply (Arteries) | Nerve Supply (Sensory) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upper Lip** | Superior Labial (from Facial art.) | Infraorbital nerve (CN V2) |
| **Lower Lip** | Inferior Labial (from Facial art.) | Mental nerve (CN V3) |
| **Cheeks** | Buccal branch of Maxillary art. | Buccal nerve (CN V3) |
| **Upper Gums** | Superior Alveolar (Maxillary art.) | Maxillary Nerve (CN V2) |
| **Lower Gums** | Inferior Alveolar (Maxillary art.) | Mandibular Nerve (CN V3) |

- **Venous Drainage:**
	- Most structures drain into:
		- **Facial vein** or the **Pterygoid venous plexus**.
- **Motor Supply:** 
    - Muscles of facial expression (Orbicularis oris, Buccinator) = **Facial Nerve (CN VII)**.
    - Muscles of mastication = **Trigeminal Nerve (CN V3)**.

---

# Summary for Quick Revision
1. **Histology Distinction:** Remember that the **lips (mucosa side)** and **cheeks** are **non-keratinized**, while the **gums** and **hard palate** are **keratinized** (masticatory mucosa).
2. **Glands:** Cheeks and lips have minor salivary glands in the submucosa; gums have **no glands**.
3. **Sensory Rule:** If it's on the top (upper lip/teeth), it’s **CN V2**. If it’s on the bottom (lower lip/teeth/cheek), it’s **CN V3**.
