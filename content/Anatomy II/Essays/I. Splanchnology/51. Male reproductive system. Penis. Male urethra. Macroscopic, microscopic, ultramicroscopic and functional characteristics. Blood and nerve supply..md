> Male reproductive system. Penis. Male urethra. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. The Penis
- **Macroscopic:** 
    - Consists of a **Root**, **Body (Shaft)**, and **Glans**.
    - Contains three cylindrical bodies of erectile tissue:
        1. **Corpora Cavernosa (2):** Paired dorsal cylinders.
        2. **Corpus Spongiosum (1):** Single ventral cylinder containing the urethra; expands at the end to form the **Glans**.

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9c/Penile_diagram.jpg/330px-Penile_diagram.jpg)

- **Microscopic:**
    - **Tunica Albuginea:** A tough, fibrous capsule surrounding the erectile bodies.
    - **Erectile Tissue:** A "sponge" of vascular sinusoids (spaces) separated by **trabeculae** (connective tissue and smooth muscle).
- **Ultramicroscopic:** 
    - Endothelial cells lining the sinusoids contain vesicles that release **Nitric Oxide (NO)** to trigger vasodilation.
- **Function:** 
    - Organ of copulation; provides a common exit for urine and semen.
- **Blood Supply:** 
    - Primarily from branches of the **Internal Pudendal Artery** (Deep arteries for erection; Dorsal arteries for the skin/glans).
- **Nerve Supply:**
    - **Parasympathetic (S2-S4):** Responsible for **Erection** (Point).
    - **Sympathetic (L1-L2):** Responsible for **Ejaculation** (Shoot).
    - **Somatic (Pudendal Nerve):** Responsible for sensation.

# 2. Male Urethra
- **Macroscopic (3 Main Parts):**
    1. **Prostatic Urethra:** Runs through the prostate gland.
    2. **Membranous Urethra:** Shortest part; passes through the urogenital diaphragm (external sphincter).
    3. **Spongy (Penile) Urethra:** Longest part; runs through the corpus spongiosum.

![|300](https://teachmeanatomy.info/wp-content/uploads/Parts-of-the-Male-Urethra..png)

- **Microscopic (Epithelium - High Yield):**
    - Changes type along its length:
        1. Prostatic: **Transitional epithelium** (urothelium).
        2. Membranous/Spongy: **Pseudostratified columnar**.
        3. Near the tip (Glans): **Stratified squamous** (non-keratinized).
    - **Glands of Littre:** Small mucus-secreting glands in the wall.
- **Ultramicroscopic:**
    - Cells have microvilli and secretory granules for mucus production to protect against acidic urine.
- **Function:** 
    - Conducts urine from the bladder; conducts semen during ejaculation.
- **Blood Supply:** 
    - Branches of the **Internal Pudendal Artery**.
- **Nerve Supply:** 
    - **Pudendal Nerve** (controls the external voluntary sphincter) and **Autonomic Plexuses** (internal sphincter).
