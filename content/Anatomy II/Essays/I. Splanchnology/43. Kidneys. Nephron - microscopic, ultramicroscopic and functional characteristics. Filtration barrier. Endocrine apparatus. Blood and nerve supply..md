> Kidneys. Nephron - microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Filtration barrier. Endocrine apparatus. Blood and nerve supply.

---

# 1. The Nephron - Microscopic
- **Composition:** Renal Corpuscle + Renal Tubules.
- **Renal Corpuscle - Filtration:** 
    - **Glomerulus:** A tuft of capillaries.
    - **Bowman’s Capsule:**
	    - Double-layered cup.
	    - Parietal layer outside, visceral layer/podocytes inside.
- **Renal Tubules - Processing:**
    - **Proximal Convoluted Tubule:**
	    - Reabsorbs 100% of glucose/amino acids and 65% of water/sodium.
	    - Microscope: Has a "brush border" of microvilli.
    - **Loop of Henle:**
	    - Concentrates urine (Descending = water out; Ascending = salt out).
    - **Distal Convoluted Tubule:**
	    - Fine-tuning of electrolytes
	    - Regulated by hormones.
    - **Collecting Duct:** Final water reabsorption (controlled by ADH).

# 2. Filtration Barrier - Ultramicroscopic
Filtration barrier is the "sieve" that keeps blood cells and proteins in the vessel while letting waste out.

It has **three layers**:
- **Fenestrated Endothelium:**
	- The capillary wall has large pores.
- **Glomerular Basement Membrane:**
	- A thick gel layer
	- It is **negatively charged** (repels proteins like Albumin).
- **Podocytes (Visceral layer):**
	- Cells with "foot processes" that wrap around capillaries.
	- The gaps between them are **filtration slits**.

# 3. Endocrine Apparatus
Located where the **DCT touches the Afferent Arteriole**.

It regulates blood pressure.
- **Macula Densa:**
	- Cells in the DCT that "taste" salt (NaCl) levels.
- **Juxtaglomerular Cells:**
	- Modified smooth muscle cells in the afferent arteriole.
	- They secrete **Renin** when BP is low.
- **Extraglomerular Mesangial Cells:**
	- Help with signaling between the other two.
- **Result:**
	- Triggers the **RAAS system** (Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone) to increase blood pressure.

# 4. Blood Supply
The kidney is highly vascular receives 20–25% of cardiac output.
1. **Renal Artery**
2. **Segmental Arteries**
3. **Interlobar Arteries** - Up between pyramids
4. **Arcuate Arteries** - Arch over the base of pyramids
5. **Interlobular Arteries** - Into the cortex
6. **Afferent Arteriole** $\rightarrow$ **Glomerulus** $\rightarrow$ **Efferent Arteriole**
7. **Peritubular Capillaries** (around tubules) / **Vasa Recta** (around Loop of Henle)
8. **Venous return** (follows the same names in reverse).

# 5. Nerve Supply
- **Source:** Renal Plexus.
- **Sympathetic:**
	- Derived from the T10–L1 spinal levels.
    - **Function:**
	    - Causes vasoconstriction (decreases blood flow and urine production)
	    - Stimulates Renin release.
- **Parasympathetic:**
	- From the Vagus nerve
	- Minimal known function.
- **Sensory (Pain):**
	- Visceral afferent fibers carry pain (e.g., kidney stones) to T10–L1 dermatomes (referred pain to the loin/groin).
