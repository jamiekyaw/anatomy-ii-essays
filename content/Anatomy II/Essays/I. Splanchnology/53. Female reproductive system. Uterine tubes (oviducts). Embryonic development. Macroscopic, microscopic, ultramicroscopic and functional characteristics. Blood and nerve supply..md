> Female reproductive system. Uterine tubes (oviducts). Embryonic development. Macroscopic, microscopic, ultramicroscopic and functional characteristics. Blood and nerve supply..

---

# 1. Embryonic Development
- **Origin:** Derived from the **Paramesonephric ducts** (also called **Müllerian ducts**).
- The cranial ends of these ducts remain open to form the openings near the ovaries.

# 2. Macroscopic Characteristics (Anatomy)
The tube is roughly 10–12 cm long and divided into four parts (from ovary to uterus):
1. **Infundibulum:** Funnel-shaped end with finger-like projections called **fimbriae** (picks up the egg).
2. **Ampulla:** The widest and longest part. **Note:** This is where **fertilization** usually happens.
3. **Isthmus:** The narrow, thick-walled section.
4. **Uterine (Intramural) part:** The segment that passes through the wall of the uterus.

# 3. Microscopic Characteristics (Histology)
The wall consists of three layers:
1. **Mucosa:** Highly folded (most folds are in the ampulla). 
2. **Muscularis:** Smooth muscle (inner circular and outer longitudinal layers) that performs peristalsis.
3. **Serosa:** The outermost layer (visceral peritoneum).

# 4. Ultramicroscopic / Epithelium
The lining is **Simple Columnar Epithelium** containing two main cell types:
- **Ciliated cells:** Have hair-like cilia that beat toward the uterus to move the egg.
- **Peg cells (Secretory cells):** Non-ciliated; they secrete fluid that nourishes the sperm and the zygote.

# 5. Functional Characteristics
- **Ovum Pickup:** Fimbriae sweep the egg into the tube after ovulation.
- **Fertilization:** Provides the site for sperm to meet the egg (Ampulla).
- **Transport:** Moves the zygote toward the uterus using cilia and muscle contractions.
- **Nourishment:** Provides nutrients for the early embryo.

# 6. Blood and Nerve Supply
- **Arteries:** Dual supply from the **Ovarian artery** (lateral) and the **Uterine artery** (medial).
- **Veins:** Drained by the Ovarian and Uterine veins.
- **Nerve Supply:** Autonomic nervous system (Sympathetic and Parasympathetic) via the **Ovarian and Hypogastric plexuses**.

**Key Exam Phrases to remember:** 
- "Fertilization happens in the **Ampulla**."
- "Developed from **Müllerian ducts**."
- "Lined by **ciliated columnar epithelium**."
