---

# 1. General Characteristics of Human Dentition
- **Thecodont:** Teeth are set in bony sockets (alveoli).
- **Diphyodont:** Two successive sets of teeth (deciduous and permanent).
- **Heterodont:** Different shapes of teeth for different functions (incisors, canines, etc.).

---

# 2. Macroscopic Anatomy (The Parts)
- **Crown:** Visible part above the gum line (covered in **Enamel**).
- **Root:** Part embedded in the jawbone (covered in **Cementum**).
- **Neck (Cervix):** The junction where the crown meets the root.
- **Pulp Cavity:** The hollow center containing nerves and blood vessels.
- **Dentin:** The hard yellow tissue making up the bulk of the tooth (under the enamel).

# 3. Functional Groups
1. **Incisors (4 per arch):** Chisel-shaped; used for **cutting**.
2. **Canines (2 per arch):** Cone-shaped/pointed; used for **tearing**.
3. **Premolars (4 per arch):** Two cusps; used for **crushing**. *(Only in permanent dentition)*.
4. **Molars (6 per arch):** Broad surfaces; used for **grinding**.

# 4. Deciduous (Primary/Milk) Dentition
- **Total Number:** 20 teeth.
- **Dental Formula (per quadrant):** 2.1.0.2 (2 Incisors, 1 Canine, 0 Premolars, 2 Molars).
- **Eruption:** Starts at ~6 months; complete by ~2.5 years.
- **Key Features:**
    - Smaller than permanent teeth.
    - Whiter in color (bluish-white).
    - Thinner enamel and dentin.
    - Roots are more flared (to make room for permanent buds).

---

# 5. Permanent (Secondary) Dentition
- **Total Number:** 32 teeth.
- **Dental Formula (per quadrant):** 2.1.2.3 (2 Incisors, 1 Canine, 2 Premolars, 3 Molars).
- **Eruption:** Starts at ~6 years (1st molar); ends at 17–21 years (3rd molar).
- **Key Features:**
    - Larger and stronger.
    - Yellowish-white color.
    - Thicker enamel.
    - Includes **Premolars** and **3rd Molars (Wisdom teeth)**, which are absent in deciduous.
