> Neck. Topographic regions. Subcutaneous elements in the cervical region.

---

# 1. General Boundaries of the Neck

**Boundaries:**
- **Superior:** Lower border of the mandible.
- **Inferior:** Clavicles and manubrium of the sternum.

# 2. Main Topographic Regions
The neck is divided into two main triangles by the **Sternocleidomastoid (SCM)** muscle:

**Main Topographic Regions:**
- **Anterior Triangle:** 
    - *Location:* In front of the SCM.
    - *Key Contents:* Thyroid gland, Larynx, Trachea, Carotid artery.
- **Posterior Triangle:** 
    - *Location:* Behind the SCM (between SCM and Trapezius).
    - *Key Contents:* Brachial plexus (nerves to the arm), Subclavian artery.

# 3. Subcutaneous (Superficial) Elements
These structures lie just under the skin, above the deep fascia.

### A. Muscle: Platysma
- A very thin, broad sheet of muscle.
- **Function:** Tenses the skin of the neck; muscle of facial expression.
- **Innervation:** Cervical branch of the **Facial Nerve (CN VII)**.

### B. Vein: External Jugular Vein (EJV)
- **Location:** Runs vertically down the neck, crossing **over** the SCM muscle.

### C. Nerves: The Cutaneous Branches of Cervical Plexus

There are four main sensory nerves that provide feeling to the skin of the neck. 

They all emerge from a single point behind the SCM called **Erb’s Point (Punctum Nervosum)**.
1. **Lesser Occipital Nerve:** Goes up to the scalp behind the ear.
2. **Great Auricular Nerve:** Goes to the skin around the ear and parotid gland.
3. **Transverse Cervical Nerve:** Goes across the front of the neck.
4. **Supraclavicular Nerves:** Go down toward the shoulder and chest.

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
1. **Landmark:** Sternocleidomastoid (SCM) divides the neck into Anterior and Posterior triangles.
2. **Superficial Muscle:** Platysma (Nerve: Facial Nerve).
3. **Superficial Vein:** External Jugular Vein (crosses the SCM).
4. **Superficial Nerves:** Erb's Point is the exit for 4 sensory nerves (Lesser Occipital, Great Auricular, Transverse Cervical, Supraclavicular).
