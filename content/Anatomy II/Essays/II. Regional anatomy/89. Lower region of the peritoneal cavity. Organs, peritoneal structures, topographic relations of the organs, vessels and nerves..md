> Lower region of the peritoneal cavity. Organs, peritoneal structures, topographic relations of the organs, vessels and nerves.

---

# 1. Definition & Boundaries
- **Location:** Everything below the **Transverse Mesocolon** (the fold of peritoneum holding the transverse colon).
- **Communication:** It connects to the upper region via the **paracolic gutters** (spaces on the sides of the colon).

# 2. The Organs
- **Small Intestine:** Jejunum (upper left) and Ileum (lower right).
- **Large Intestine:** Cecum, Appendix, Ascending Colon, Descending Colon, and Sigmoid Colon.

# 3. Peritoneal Structures (The "Folds")
- **The Mesentery (Proper):** A fan-shaped fold that attaches the small intestine to the back wall. It carries blood vessels and nerves.
- **Mesosigmoid:** The fold holding the Sigmoid Colon.
- **Paracolic Gutters (Right and Left):** Channels between the colon and the abdominal wall. 
- **Mesenteric Sinuses (Right and Left):** The spaces on either side of the Mesentery Proper.

# 4. Topographic Relations (Positioning)
- **Right Side:** Cecum and Ascending colon (Retroperitoneal – fixed to the back wall).
- **Left Side:** Descending colon (Retroperitoneal) and Sigmoid colon (Intraperitoneal – moves freely).
- **Central:** Coils of the small intestine.
- **Posteriorly (Behind the peritoneum):** Ureters, Great vessels (Aorta/IVC), and Kidneys.

# 5. Vessels (Blood Supply)
- **Superior Mesenteric Artery (SMA):** Supplies the "Midgut" (Small intestine, Cecum, Ascending colon, and the first 2/3 of the Transverse colon).
- **Inferior Mesenteric Artery (IMA):** Supplies the "Hindgut" (Last 1/3 of Transverse colon, Descending colon, Sigmoid, and Rectum).
- **Venous Drainage:** All blood goes to the **Portal Vein** (to the liver).

# 6. Nerves
- **Sympathetic:** Lesser and Least Splanchnic nerves (slows digestion).
- **Parasympathetic:** 
    - **Vagus Nerve:** Supplies everything up to the end of the midgut.
    - **Pelvic Splanchnic Nerves:** Supplies the hindgut (descending/sigmoid colon).

---

# The "Cheat Sheet" Summary for Passing:
1. **Divide it:** Use the Transverse Mesocolon as the roof.
2. **The Small Bowel:** Held by "The Mesentery."
3. **The Colon:** Ascending/Descending are fixed (back wall); Sigmoid is mobile.
4. **The Gutters:** Paracolic gutters allow fluid to move up and down the abdomen.
5. **The Arteries:** SMA = Right side/Small bowel; IMA = Left side.
