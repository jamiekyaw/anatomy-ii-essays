> Diaphagm.

---

# 1. What is it?
- The primary muscle of **Inspiration** (breathing in).
- A dome-shaped sheet that separates the **Thorax** (chest) from the **Abdomen** (belly).

![|400](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8b/Respiratory_system.svg)

# 2. The 3 Major Openings (Must Know)
Exams always ask which structures pass through at which vertebral level. Use the mnemonic **"I Eat Apples"**:

1.  **T8 (Vena Cava Opening):** 
    - Passes: **I**VC (Inferior Vena Cava).
2.  **T10 (Esophageal Hiatus):** 
    - Passes: **E**sophagus and Vagus Nerve.
3.  **T12 (Aortic Hiatus):** 
    - Passes: **A**orta, Azygos vein, and Thoracic duct.

# 3. Nerve Supply (Innervation)
- **The Phrenic Nerve.**
- **Root Values:** **C3, C4, C5.**
- **Mnemonic:** "C3, 4, 5 keep the diaphragm alive."
- *Note:* If the phrenic nerve is cut, the diaphragm is paralyzed.

# 4. Shape and Structure
- **Right Dome:** Higher than the left (because the **Liver** sits right under it).
- **Central Tendon:** The middle part of the diaphragm is made of tough tendon, not muscle.
- **Crura:** Two muscular "legs" (Left and Right Crus) that attach it to the lumbar spine.

# 5. Action
- **Contraction:** The diaphragm flattens and moves down. This increases chest volume, drawing air into the lungs.
- **Relaxation:** The diaphragm moves up (dome shape returns). This pushes air out of the lungs.
