# 1. THE TEMPORAL REGION (The Temple)

**Key Muscle:**
- **Temporalis Muscle:** A large fan-shaped muscle.
- **Function:** Closes the jaw (elevates mandible).
- **Nerve:** Mandibular Nerve (V3).

**The Pterion (CRITICAL POINT):**
- An H-shaped junction where four bones meet (Frontal, Parietal, Temporal, Sphenoid).

**Vessels & Nerves:**
- **Superficial Temporal Artery:** You can feel your pulse here (in front of the ear).
- **Auriculotemporal Nerve:** Provides sensation to the side of the head.

# 2. THE BUCCAL REGION (The Cheek)

**Key Muscle:**
- **Buccinator Muscle:** The main muscle of the cheek.
- **Function:** Flattens the cheek against teeth (keeps food in place while chewing, or used for whistling/blowing).
- **Nerve:** **Facial Nerve (CN VII)** provides motor movement.

**Key Structure:**
- **Parotid Duct (Stensen’s Duct):** 
	- This tube carries saliva. It **pierces** through the buccinator muscle to open inside the mouth near the 2nd upper molar.

**Key Content:**
- **Buccal Fat Pad:** A collection of fat that gives the cheek its rounded shape (very prominent in infants for sucking).

# SUMMARY FOR THE EXAM (Cheat Sheet)
- **Temporal muscle** = Chewing (V3 nerve).
- **Pterion** = Thin bone + Middle Meningeal Artery + Hematoma risk.
- **Buccinator muscle** = Whistling/Chewing (Facial nerve).
- **Parotid Duct** = Pierces the buccinator.
- **Pulse point** = Superficial temporal artery (in front of ear).
