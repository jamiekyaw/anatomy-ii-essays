> Infrahyoid region.

---

# 1. The Muscles (The "Strap" Muscles)
There are four muscles located below the hyoid bone. They are arranged in two layers.

**Superficial Layer:**
- **Sternohyoid:** From sternum to hyoid.
- **Omohyoid:** From scapula to hyoid (has two bellies joined by a tendon).

**Deep Layer:**
- **Sternothyroid:** From sternum to thyroid cartilage.
- **Thyrohyoid:** From thyroid cartilage to hyoid.

**Function:** All four muscles **depress the hyoid bone** (pull it down) after it has been raised during swallowing or speaking.

# 2. Innervation (The "High-Yield" Point)
This is the most common exam question for this region.

- **Ansa Cervicalis (C1-C3):** Supplies 3 out of the 4 muscles (Sternohyoid, Sternothyroid, Omohyoid).
- **The Exception:** The **Thyrohyoid** muscle is supplied by **C1 fibers via the Hypoglossal nerve (CN XII)**.

---

# 3. Blood Supply
- Mainly supplied by the **Superior and Inferior Thyroid arteries**.
- Venous drainage is via the **Anterior Jugular veins**.

---

# 4. Relationships (What’s underneath?)
The infrahyoid muscles act as a "shield" or cover for deeper structures:
- **Thyroid Gland:** Located deep to the sternothyroid and sternohyoid.
- **Trachea:** Located in the midline, deep to these muscles.
- **Larynx:** Specifically the thyroid and cricoid cartilages.

---

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
1. **Name the 4 muscles:** Sternohyoid, Sternothyroid, Omohyoid, Thyrohyoid.
2. **Action:** Depress the hyoid bone.
3. **Nerve:** Ansa Cervicalis (mostly).
4. **The "Trap" answer:** Thyrohyoid is C1 via Hypoglossal nerve.
