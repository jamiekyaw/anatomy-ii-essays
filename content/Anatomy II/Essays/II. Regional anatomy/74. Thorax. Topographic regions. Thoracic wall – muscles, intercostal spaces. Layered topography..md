> Thorax. Topographic regions. Thoracic wall – muscles, intercostal spaces. Layered topography.

---

# 1. Topographic Lines (Imaginary vertical lines)
- **Midsternal line:** Passes through the center of the sternum.
- **Midclavicular line:** Drops vertically from the midpoint of the clavicle.
- **Axillary lines:** Anterior, Mid, and Posterior axillary lines (defined by the armpit).
- **Scapular line:** Passes through the inferior angle of the scapula.
- **Sternal Angle (Angle of Louis):** The most important landmark. It is at the level of the **T4/T5** vertebrae and the **2nd rib**.

# 2. The Thoracic Wall: Muscles
The wall is mainly formed by the **Intercostal Muscles** (3 layers):
1. **External Intercostal:** Fibers run down and forward ("hands in pockets"). Function: **Inspiration** (elevates ribs).
2. **Internal Intercostal:** Fibers run down and backward. Function: **Expiration** (depresses ribs).
3. **Innermost Intercostal:** Deepest layer, separated from the Internal by the neurovascular bundle.

- **The Diaphragm:** The floor of the thorax. The primary muscle for breathing.

# 3. The Intercostal Space (ICS)
There are 11 intercostal spaces. Every space contains a **Neurovascular Bundle**.
- **Order (Top to Bottom):** **V—A—N** 
    - **V**ein
    - **A**rtery
    - **N**erve
- **Location:** They sit in the **costal groove** at the **inferior (bottom) border** of the upper rib.

---

# 4. Layered Topography (Surface to Deep)
If you were to push a needle through the chest wall into the lung, you would pass through:
1. **Skin**
2. **Superficial fascia** (contains fat and mammary glands)
3. **Deep fascia** (covering the muscles)
4. **Extrinsic muscles** (e.g., Pectoralis major/Serratus anterior)
5. **External intercostal muscle**
6. **Internal intercostal muscle**
7. **Neurovascular bundle (VAN)**
8. **Innermost intercostal muscle**
9. **Endothoracic fascia** (the "glue" between the wall and the pleura)
10. **Parietal pleura** (outer lining of the lung cavity)
11. **Pleural cavity** (potential space)
12. **Visceral pleura** (lining on the lung itself)
13. **Lung**

---

# 5. Blood Supply & Innervation
- **Arteries:** Derived from the **Internal Thoracic Artery** (anteriorly) and the **Thoracic Aorta** (posteriorly).
- **Nerves:** **Intercostal Nerves** (derived from the anterior rami of T1–T11 spinal nerves).

# Summary "Must-Knows" to Pass:
1. **Sternal Angle** = 2nd Rib / T4–T5 level.
2. **VAN** = Order of structures in the rib groove (Vein, Artery, Nerve).
3. **External Intercostals** = Inspiration / **Internal** = Expiration.
4. **Pleura** = Two layers (Parietal and Visceral) with a cavity in between.
