> Peripharyngeal region

---

# 1. Shape & Location
- **Shape:** An inverted pyramid.
- **Location:** Lateral to the pharynx and medial to the mandible/parotid gland.
- **Extent:** From the **base of the skull** to the **hyoid bone**.

![|500](https://i0.wp.com/entokey.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/10-1055-b-004-135640_c003_f001.jpg?w=960)

# 2. Boundaries (The "Box")
- **Medial (Inside):** Pharynx (Superior constrictor muscle).
- **Lateral (Outside):** Mandible (Ramus) and the Parotid gland.
- **Posterior (Back):** Prevertebral fascia (neck muscles).

# 3. Key Division
The space is divided into two parts by the **Styloid Process** and its muscles:
1. **Pre-styloid** (in front of the styloid).
2. **Post-styloid** (behind the styloid).

# 4. High-Yield Contents
*This is the most likely part to be tested.*

- **Pre-styloid compartment:**
    - Fat (mostly).
    - Deep lobe of the **Parotid gland**.
    - Internal maxillary artery.
- **Post-styloid compartment (The "Vitals"):**
    - **Carotid Sheath Includes: 
	    - Internal Carotid Artery
	    - Internal Jugular Vein.
    - **Cranial Nerves:** IX (Glossopharyngeal), X (Vagus), XI (Accessory), and XII (Hypoglossal).
    - **Sympathetic Trunk.**
