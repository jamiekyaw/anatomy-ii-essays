These are the high-yield, barebones notes needed to pass this topic.

# 1. Superior Vena Cava (SVC)
- **Position:** Located in the **superior mediastinum**. It is formed by the union of the Right and Left **Brachiocephalic veins** behind the 1st right costal cartilage. It ends at the **Right Atrium** (level of 3rd costal cartilage).
- **Main Tributaries:** 
    1. Right Brachiocephalic vein.
    2. Left Brachiocephalic vein.
    3. **Azygos vein** (enters the SVC just before it reaches the heart).

# 2. Inferior Vena Cava (IVC)
- **Position:** Located in the **posterior abdominal cavity**, to the right of the aorta. It is formed by the union of the Right and Left **Common Iliac veins** at the level of **L5**. It pierces the diaphragm at **T8** and enters the **Right Atrium**.
- **Main Tributaries:**
    1. Common Iliac veins.
    2. **Lumbar veins.**
    3. **Renal veins** (Left and Right).
    4. Right Suprarenal and Right Gonadal veins (Note: Left ones drain into the Left Renal vein first).
    5. **Hepatic veins.**

# 3. Cavo-Caval Anastomoses (Backups)
*These are connections between the SVC and IVC. If one is blocked, blood uses these routes to get back to the heart.*

1. **Azygos System:** The Azygos and Hemiazygos veins connect the IVC (via lumbar veins) to the SVC.
2. **Vertebral Venous Plexus (Batson’s):** Veins running inside and outside the spinal column.
3. **Anterior Abdominal Wall:** The **Thoracoepigastric vein** connects the Lateral Thoracic vein (SVC system) to the Superficial Epigastric vein (IVC system).
