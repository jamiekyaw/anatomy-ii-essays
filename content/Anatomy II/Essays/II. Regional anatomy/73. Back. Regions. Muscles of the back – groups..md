# 1. Regions of the Back
The back is divided based on the vertebral column:
- **Cervical:** Neck region.
- **Thoracic:** Upper and middle back (where ribs attach).
- **Lumbar:** Lower back (the "small" of the back).
- **Sacral:** Base of the spine/pelvis area.

# 2. Muscle Groups
Muscles are divided into two main categories: **Extrinsic** and **Intrinsic**.

## A. Extrinsic Muscles (Superficial & Intermediate)
*These help move the arms and the ribcage.*

1.  **Trapezius:** Large diamond-shaped muscle. 
    - *Function:* Shrugging shoulders.
2.  **Latissimus Dorsi:** Large muscle of the lower back. 
    - *Function:* Pulling the arm down and back (swimming motion).
3.  **Rhomboids (Major/Minor):** Between the shoulder blades. 
    - *Function:* Pulling shoulder blades together (retraction).
4.  **Levator Scapulae:** 
    - *Function:* Lifts the shoulder blade.
5.  **Serratus Posterior:** 
    - *Function:* Involved in breathing (respiration).

## B. Intrinsic Muscles (Deep)
*These are the "true" back muscles. They support the spine and posture.*

1.  **Erector Spinae Group:** (The most important deep group). 
    - Consists of 3 muscles: **Iliocostalis, Longissimus, Spinalis**.
    - *Function:* Keeps the spine straight (extension).
2.  **Splenius Muscles:** Located in the neck.
    - *Function:* Rotate and extend the head.
3.  **Transversospinalis Group:** Very deep, small muscles between vertebrae.
    - *Function:* Stabilization and tiny rotations of the spine.

# 3. High-Yield "Cheat Sheet" Points
If you only remember four things, remember these:

- **Trapezius =** Shrugging.
- **Latissimus Dorsi =** Pulling/Climbing.
- **Erector Spinae =** Standing up straight/Back extension.
- **Innervation:** Most **deep** back muscles are supplied by **Posterior (Dorsal) Rami** of spinal nerves. (The superficial ones are mostly supplied by anterior rami).
