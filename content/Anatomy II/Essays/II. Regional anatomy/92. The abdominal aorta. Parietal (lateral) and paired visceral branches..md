> The abdominal aorta. Parietal (lateral) and paired visceral branches.

---

# The Abdominal Aorta: Basics
- **Start:** T12 (Aortic hiatus of diaphragm).
- **End:** L4 (Bifurcates into Left and Right Common Iliac Arteries).

![|300](https://geekymedics.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/aorta-geeky-medics.jpg)

---

# 1. Parietal Branches
These are generally paired and **supply the body wall**.

1. **Inferior Phrenic Arteries:** Supply the underside of the diaphragm.
2. **Lumbar Arteries:** Usually 4 pairs. Supply the posterior abdominal wall and spinal cord.
3. **Median Sacral Artery:** (Unpaired) Runs down the middle of the sacrum to the coccyx.

---

# 2. Paired Visceral Branches
They **supply organs**. Listed from top to bottom:

1. **Middle Suprarenal Arteries:** Supply the adrenal (suprarenal) glands.
2. **Renal Arteries (Level L1/L2):** Supply the kidneys. (Note: The Right Renal artery is longer and passes behind the IVC).
3. **Gonadal Arteries (Level L2):** 
    - In males: **Testicular arteries**.
    - In females: **Ovarian arteries**.

---

# Summary Table for Easy Recall

| Branch Type | Name | Supplies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parietal** | Inferior Phrenic | Diaphragm |
| **Parietal** | Lumbar (4 pairs) | Back/Abdominal wall |
| **Visceral** | Middle Suprarenal | Adrenal glands |
| **Visceral** | Renal | Kidneys |
| **Visceral** | Gonadal | Testes or Ovaries |

**One "Pro" Tip for the Pass:**
If asked for the levels, remember **L1 (Renals)** and **L2 (Gonadals)**. These are the most common follow-up questions.
