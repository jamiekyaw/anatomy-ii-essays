> Thoracic cavity. Pleura. Pleural cavity. Projections of the pleura.

---

# 1. The Thoracic Cavity
- **Definition:** The space within the chest protected by the rib cage.
- **Boundaries:**
    - **Superior:** Thoracic inlet (neck).
    - **Inferior:** Diaphragm.
    - **Lateral:** Ribs and intercostal muscles.
- **Contents:** Divided into three parts:
    1. **Mediastinum:** Central compartment (contains heart, esophagus, trachea).
    2. **Right Pulmonary Cavity:** Contains Right Lung.
    3. **Left Pulmonary Cavity:** Contains Left Lung.

# 2. The Pleura
The pleura is a **serous membrane** (double-layered sac) surrounding each lung.
1. **Visceral Pleura:** Covers the surface of the **lung** (cannot be separated from the lung).
2. **Parietal Pleura:** Lines the **thoracic wall** and diaphragm.
- **Innervation (Crucial for exams):**
    - **Parietal Pleura:** Sensitive to **pain** (via Intercostal and Phrenic nerves).
    - **Visceral Pleura:** Sensitive only to stretch (Autonomic nerves); **no pain** sensation.

---

# 3. The Pleural Cavity
- **Definition:** The "potential space" between the visceral and parietal pleura.
- **Pleural Fluid:** A thin film of liquid that reduces friction and provides surface tension (keeps lungs stuck to the chest wall).
- **Pleural Recesses:** Areas where the cavity is larger than the lung, allowing for lung expansion.
    - **Costodiaphragmatic Recess:** The most important one. It is the lowest point; fluid collects here first (Pleural Effusion).

# 4. Projections of the Pleura (Surface Anatomy)
To pass, remember the **"Rule of 2s"** to distinguish the **Pleura** from the **Lung**.

**Where the Pleura ends (Bottom border):**
- **Midclavicular line:** Rib **8**
- **Midaxillary line:** Rib **10**
- **Paravertebral (back) line:** Rib **12**

*(Note: The **Lung** ends 2 ribs higher: Ribs 6, 8, and 10 respectively).*

**Other Key Projections:**
- **Cervical Pleura (Apex):** Extends into the neck, about 2–3 cm above the medial 1/3 of the **clavicle**.
- **Sternal line:** Right and Left pleura meet at the midline behind the sternum, but the **Left** pleura deviates at the 4th rib to accommodate the heart (Cardiac notch).

---

# Summary Checklist for Exam:
- **Parietal pleura** = Pain. **Visceral** = No pain.
- **Negative pressure** in the pleural cavity keeps lungs inflated.
- **Costodiaphragmatic recess** = Where fluid goes.
- **Pleural levels** = 8, 10, 12 (Ribs).
