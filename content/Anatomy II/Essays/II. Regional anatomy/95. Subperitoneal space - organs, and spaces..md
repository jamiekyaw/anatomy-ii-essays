> Subperitoneal space - organs, and spaces.

---

# 1. Definition
- The space between the **pelvic peritoneum** (ceiling) and the **levator ani muscles** (floor).
- It is continuous with the retroperitoneal space of the abdomen.

# 2. Key Organs (The "Must-Knows")
If asked for organs in this space, list these:
- **Urinary:** Urinary bladder, lower ends of the Ureters.
- **Genital (Male):** Prostate gland, Seminal vesicles, Vas deferens.
- **Genital (Female):** Cervix of the uterus, upper Vagina.
- **Digestive:** Rectum (lower 2/3rds).

# 3. Key Spaces (Potential Gaps)
These are "potential" spaces filled with loose connective tissue and fat:
- **Retropubic Space:** Between the pubic bone and the urinary bladder.
- **Paravesical Spaces:** Located on either side of the bladder.
- **Pararectal Spaces:** Located on either side of the rectum.
- **Rectovesical / Rectovaginal Septum:** The connective tissue layer separating the rectum from the bladder (males) or vagina (females).

# 4. Contents (Other than Organs)
- **Internal Iliac Artery/Vein:** And their branches.
- **Pelvic Plexus:** Autonomic nerves (hypogastric nerves).
- **Lymph nodes:** Internal iliac and sacral nodes.
