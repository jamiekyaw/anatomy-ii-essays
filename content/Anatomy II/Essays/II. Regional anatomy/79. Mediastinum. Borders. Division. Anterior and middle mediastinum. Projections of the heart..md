# 2. Borders
- **Superior:** Thoracic inlet (opening to the neck).
- **Inferior:** Diaphragm.
- **Anterior:** Sternum.
- **Posterior:** Thoracic vertebral bodies (T1–T12).
- **Lateral:** Mediastinal pleura (the lungs).

# 3. Divisions
The mediastinum is divided into **Superior** and **Inferior** by an imaginary horizontal line called the **Transthoracic Plane** (or Plane of Ludwig).

- **The Plane:** Runs from the **Sternal Angle** (Angle of Louis) to the **T4/T5 intervertebral disc**.

**The Inferior Mediastinum is further split into 3 parts:**
1. **Anterior:** Between sternum and heart.
2. **Middle:** Contains the heart and its sac (pericardium).
3. **Posterior:** Between the heart and the spine.

# 3. Anterior and Middle Mediastinum

**Anterior Mediastinum:**
- **Location:** Between the sternum and the pericardium.
- **Contents:**
    - Thymus gland (in children) or fatty remnants (in adults).
    - Lymph nodes.
    - Internal thoracic vessels.

**Middle Mediastinum:**
- **Location:** The central portion of the inferior mediastinum.
- **Contents:**
    - **The Heart** and the **Pericardium**.
    - Roots of Great Vessels (Ascending aorta, Superior Vena Cava, Pulmonary trunk).
    - **Phrenic nerves** (pass right through here to the diaphragm).
    - Main bronchi.

# 5. Projections of the Heart
To map the heart on the chest wall, remember these four points:

1. **Superior Right Point:** 3rd costal cartilage (1 cm from sternal edge).
2. **Superior Left Point:** 2nd intercostal space (2 cm from sternal edge).
3. **Inferior Right Point:** 6th costal cartilage (1 cm from sternal edge).
4. **Inferior Left Point (Apex):** 5th intercostal space (9 cm from the midline, or the midclavicular line).

![](https://d45jl3w9libvn.cloudfront.net/jaypee/static/books/9789350253823/Chapters/images/415-2.jpg)

**Summary for the exam:** 
- **Apex** = Left 5th intercostal space.
- **Middle Mediastinum** = Heart & Phrenic nerve.
- **Division Level** = Sternal Angle (T4/T5).
