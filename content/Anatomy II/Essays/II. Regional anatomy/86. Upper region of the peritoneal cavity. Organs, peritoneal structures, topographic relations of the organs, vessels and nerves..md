> Upper region of the peritoneal cavity. Organs, peritoneal structures, topographic relations of the organs, vessels and nerves.

---

# 1. General Boundaries
- **Superior:** Diaphragm.
- **Inferior:** Transverse colon and its mesentery (Transverse Mesocolon).
- **Contents:** Liver, Stomach, Spleen, Pancreas (partially), Gallbladder, and part of the Duodenum.

# 2. The Main Organs
1. **Liver:** Located in the Right Hypochondrium. Has 4 lobes (Right, Left, Caudate, Quadrate).
2. **Stomach:** J-shaped. Key parts: Fundus, Body, Antrum, Pylorus.
3. **Spleen:** Located in the Left Hypochondrium (ribs 9–11).
4. **Gallbladder:** Under the liver; stores bile.

# 3. Peritoneal Structures (Omenta & Spaces)
- **Lesser Omentum:** Connects Liver to Stomach. Contains the "Portal Triad."
- **Greater Omentum:** "The Policeman of the abdomen." Hangs from the Greater Curvature of the stomach.
- **Lesser Sac (Omental Bursa):** A potential space **behind** the stomach.
- **Epiploic Foramen (of Winslow):** The only opening/entrance into the Lesser Sac.
    - *Boundaries:* Portal vein (front), IVC (back), Liver (top), Duodenum (bottom).

---

# 4. Topographic Relations (Who is next to whom?)
- **Stomach:** In front of the Pancreas (Pancreas is in the "stomach bed").
- **Liver:** To the right of the stomach; sits under the diaphragm.
- **Spleen:** To the left of the stomach.
- **Pancreas:** Sits transversely behind the stomach. Its head is tucked into the C-shape of the duodenum.

---

# 5. Blood Vessels (The Celiac Trunk)
The **Celiac Trunk** (level of T12) supplies the entire upper region. It has **3 main branches**:
1. **Left Gastric Artery:** Supplies the lesser curvature of the stomach.
2. **Splenic Artery:** Runs behind the stomach to the spleen (tortuous/wiggly path).
3. **Common Hepatic Artery:** Goes toward the liver.
    - *Divides into:* Proper Hepatic (to liver) and Gastroduodenal (to duodenum/pancreas).

**Venous Drainage:**
- All blood goes to the **Portal Vein** (formed by the union of the Splenic Vein and Superior Mesenteric Vein). It carries blood to the liver.

---

# 6. Nerves
- **Parasympathetic:** **Vagus Nerve (CN X)**. Stimulates secretion and motility (digestion).
- **Sympathetic:** **Celiac Plexus** (Greater Splanchnic Nerves T5–T9). Inhibits digestion and carries pain signals.

---

# "Must-Know" Summary for Passing:
1. **Compartment:** Above the transverse mesocolon.
2. **Artery:** Celiac Trunk.
3. **Vein:** Portal Vein.
4. **Space:** Lesser sac is behind the stomach.
5. **Entrance:** Foramen of Winslow.
6. **Triad:** Portal vein, Hepatic artery, and Bile duct are inside the Lesser Omentum.
