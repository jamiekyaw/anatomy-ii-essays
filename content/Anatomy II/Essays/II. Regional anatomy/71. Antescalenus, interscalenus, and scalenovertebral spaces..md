> Antescalenus, interscalenus, and scalenovertebral spaces.

---

# 1. Antescalenus Space (Pre-scalenic Space)
Located in front of the Scalenus anterior muscle.

- **Boundaries:**
    - **Anterior:** Sternocleidomastoid muscle.
    - **Posterior:** Scalenus anterior muscle.
    - **Inferior:** 1st Rib.
- **Key Contents:** 
    - **Subclavian VEIN** (Note: The vein is in *front* of the muscle).
    - **Phrenic Nerve.**

# 2. Interscalenus Space (Scalene Fissure)
The gap between the anterior and middle scalene muscles. **(High Yield: Most commonly tested).**

- **Boundaries:**
    - **Anterior:** Scalenus anterior muscle.
    - **Posterior:** Scalenus medius muscle.
    - **Inferior:** 1st Rib.
- **Key Contents:**
    - **Subclavian ARTERY** (Note: The artery is *behind* the muscle).
    - **Brachial Plexus** (Trunks).

# 3. Scalenovertebral Space (Scalenovertebral Triangle)
A pyramid-shaped space located deeper and more medially.

- **Boundaries:**
    - **Lateral:** Scalenus anterior muscle.
    - **Medial:** Longus colli muscle (vertebral column).
    - **Posterior:** Transverse processes of C6–C7.
    - **Apex:** Transverse process of C6 (Chassaignac tubercle).
    - **Base (Inferior):** Cupula of the pleura (apex of the lung).
- **Key Contents:**
    - **Subclavian Artery** (1st part).
    - **Vertebral Artery** (entering the C6 foramen).
    - **Thoracic Duct** (on the **Left** side only).
    - **Sympathetic Trunk** (specifically the Stellate/Cervicothoracic ganglion).

# Summary Cheat Sheet for Passing:
- **Antescalenus:** Subclavian **Vein** + Phrenic Nerve.
- **Interscalenus:** Subclavian **Artery** + **Brachial Plexus**.
- **Scalenovertebral:** **Vertebral Artery** + Thoracic Duct (Left).
