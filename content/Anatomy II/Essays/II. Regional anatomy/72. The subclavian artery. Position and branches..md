> The subclavian artery. Position and branches.

---

# 1. Origin (Where it starts)
- **Right Subclavian:** Brachiocephalic trunk.
- **Left Subclavian:** Arch of the aorta.

# 2. Parts (The Landmark)
The artery is divided into **three parts** by the **Scalenus Anterior muscle**:
1.  **First part:** Medial to the muscle.
2.  **Second part:** Behind the muscle.
3.  **Third part:** Lateral to the muscle.

![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/wPe4lEKUoAE/maxresdefault.jpg)

# 3. Termination (Where it ends)
- It ends at the **outer (lateral) border of the 1st rib**.
- At this point, its name changes to the **Axillary Artery**.

# 4. Branches (The "VIT C & D" Mnemonic)
Most branches come from the **1st part**:

- **V: Vertebral artery** (goes to the brain).
- **I: Internal thoracic artery** (goes down the inside of the chest).
- **T: Thyrocervical trunk** (supplies the thyroid and neck).
    - *Key sub-branches: Inferior thyroid, Suprascapular, Transverse cervical.*

From the **2nd part**:
- **C: Costocervical trunk** (supplies upper ribs/neck).

From the **3rd part**:
- **D: Dorsal scapular artery** (supplies the back/scapula).

# Summary for Examiners
- **Landmark:** Scalenus Anterior muscle.
- **Boundary:** Becomes Axillary artery at the 1st rib.
- **Main function:** Primary blood supply to the upper limb and brain (via vertebral artery).
