> Mediastinum. Borders. Division. Superior mediastinum.

---

# 1. Definition
The **mediastinum** is the central compartment of the thoracic cavity (the space between the two lungs).

---

# 2. Borders
- **Superior:** Thoracic inlet (opening to the neck).
- **Inferior:** Diaphragm.
- **Anterior:** Sternum.
- **Posterior:** Thoracic vertebral bodies (T1–T12).
- **Lateral:** Mediastinal pleura (the lungs).

---

# 3. Divisions
The mediastinum is divided into **Superior** and **Inferior** by an imaginary horizontal line called the **Transthoracic Plane** (or Plane of Ludwig).

- **The Plane:** Runs from the **Sternal Angle** (Angle of Louis) to the **T4/T5 intervertebral disc**.

**The Inferior Mediastinum is further split into 3 parts:**
1. **Anterior:** Between sternum and heart.
2. **Middle:** Contains the heart and its sac (pericardium).
3. **Posterior:** Between the heart and the spine.

---

# 4. Superior Mediastinum
This is the area **above** the T4/T5 plane. 

**Key Contents (from Anterior to Posterior):**
- **Thymus:** Lymphatic organ; large in kids, fatty in adults.
- **Veins:** Superior Vena Cava and Brachiocephalic veins.
- **Arteries:** Arch of the Aorta and its 3 branches:
    1. Brachiocephalic trunk.
    2. Left common carotid artery.
    3. Left subclavian artery.
- **Nerves:** Phrenic nerves and Vagus nerves.
- **Airway:** Trachea.
- **Alimentary:** Esophagus.
- **Duct:** Thoracic duct.

---

# 5. Summary Cheat Sheet for Passing
- **Where is it?** Between the lungs.
- **The "Magic Line":** T4/T5 (Sternal angle). This separates Superior from Inferior.
- **Middle Mediastinum:** Always means the **Heart**.
- **Superior Mediastinum Contents:** Great vessels (Aorta/SVC), Trachea, Esophagus.
