> The hepatic portal system - constituting veins. Position of the portal vein. Anastomoses between the portal and systematic circulation.

---

# 1. What is the Hepatic Portal System?
- **Definition:**
	- A system of veins that drains blood from the GI tract and spleen into the **liver** (not directly to the heart).
- **Main Function:**
	- To deliver nutrients and toxins from food to the liver for processing before reaching the rest of the body.

![](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/3-s2.0-B9780128042540000107-f10-25-9780128042540.jpg)

# 2. The Main Veins (Formation)
The Portal Vein is formed by the union of two main vessels:
1. **Superior Mesenteric Vein (SMV):** Drains the small intestine and half of the large intestine.
2. **Splenic Vein:** Drains the spleen.
- *Note:* The **Inferior Mesenteric Vein (IMV)** usually drains into the Splenic vein first.

![](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/3-s2.0-B9780128042540000107-f10-25-9780128042540.jpg)

# 3. Position of the Portal Vein
- **Origin:** Formed behind the **neck of the pancreas**.
- **Vertebral Level:** L2 level.
- **Path:** It travels upwards within the **hepatoduodenal ligament** (part of the lesser omentum).
- **Termination:** Enters the liver at the **Porta Hepatis** (the "gateway" of the liver).

# 4. Portosystemic Anastomoses (The "Big Three")
These are places where the Portal system meets the Systemic (Vena Cava) system. If the liver is scarred (Cirrhosis), blood backs up here.

| Site | Portal Vein Involved | Systemic Vein Involved | Clinical Sign (if blocked) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lower Esophagus** | Left Gastric Vein | Azygos Vein | **Esophageal Varices** (vomiting blood) |
| **Umbilicus** | Paraumbilical Veins | Superficial Epigastric Veins | **Caput Medusae** (distended veins around navel) |
| **Rectum / Anal Canal** | Superior Rectal Vein | Middle/Inferior Rectal Veins | **Anorectal Varices** (internal hemorrhoids) |

# Summary Checklist for Passing:
- **Portal Vein =** Splenic Vein + Superior Mesenteric Vein.
- **Location =** Behind the neck of the pancreas.
- **Liver Entry =** Porta Hepatis.
- **Key Clinical Signs =** Esophageal Varices and Caput Medusae.
